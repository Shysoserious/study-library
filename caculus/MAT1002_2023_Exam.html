Question 1[27 pts]Short questions — no intermediate step required.
  1. Suppose that we substitute spherical coordinates $$x=\rho\sin\varphi\cos\theta,\quad y=\rho\sin\varphi\sin\theta,\quad z=\rho\cos\varphi$$ in a differentiable function $w=f(x,y,z)$. Find $\dfrac{\partial w}{\partial \theta}$.
  2. Let $f(x,y,z)=x^2+\tfrac{1}{2}y^2+z^2$. Find the unit direction in which $f$ increases the most rapidly at the point $P=(1,1,1)$.
  3. Consider $f$ and $P$ in (ii). Find the directional derivative of $f$ at $P$ in the direction of $\langle 0,2,-1\rangle$.
  4. Find the quadratic approximation of $f(x,y)=2\cos x\cos y$ centered at the origin according to Taylor's formula.
  5. True (T) or False (F)? The following integral exists as a real number: $$\iint_{x^2+y^2\le 1}\frac{1}{x^2+y^2}\,dA.$$
  6. Rewrite the integral $$\int_0^1\!\!\int_{-1}^0\!\!\int_0^{y^2} dx\,dy\,dz$$ as an equivalent iterated integral in the order of $dz\,dx\,dy$.
  7. Suppose $f(x,y,z)=x^2+y+yz$. Then $\operatorname{div}(\nabla f)=\nabla\cdot(\nabla f)=(\;\;)$.
  8. Find the first four nonzero terms in the Taylor series of $$\dfrac{x}{\sqrt[3]{1+x}}$$ centered at $0$.
  9. True (T) or False (F)? If a convergent alternating series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n u_n$ satisfies $u_n>0$ for all $n$ and $\lim_{n\to\infty}u_n=0$, then $\{u_n\}$ will be eventually nonincreasing, that is, there exists $N$ such that $$u_N\ge u_{N+1}\ge u_{N+2}\ge\cdots$$
Question 2[15 pts]Determine the following limits. If a limit exists (as a real number), find its value and show steps; otherwise, explain why it does not exist.
  1. $\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\dfrac{\sin x+\cos y}{x+y}.$
  2. $\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\dfrac{x^3-y^3}{x^2+y^2}.$
  3. $\displaystyle\lim_{(r,\theta)\to(1,0)}\dfrac{r\sin\theta}{r^2-1}.$
Question 3[6 pts]

Let $$f(x,y)=\begin{cases}y^3,& y\ge 0\\ -y^2,& y<0\end{cases}$$ be defined for all $(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^2$. Find $f_{xx}$, $f_{xy}$, and $f_{yy}$, and state the domain for each second-order partial derivative above.

Question 4[6 pts]

Consider the surface $S$ given by $x^2+2y^2+z^2=4$. Find the tangent plane $M$ and normal line $L$ to the surface $S$ at the point $(1,1,1)$.

Question 5[6 pts]

Find the point on the plane $2x+y-z=4$ that has minimum distance from the origin $(0,0,0)$.

Question 6[8 pts]

Suppose that the temperature (in degree Celsius) at the point $(x,y,z)$ is given by $T(x,y,z)=xyz^2$. Find the points of highest and lowest temperature on the sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2=4$.

Question 7[6+6 pts]Evaluate the following integrals.
  1. $\displaystyle\iint_R (2x-1-y)\,dA$, where $R$ is the region bounded by the curve $y=2\sqrt{x}$, the $y$-axis, and the line $y=4x-2$.
  2. $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}\dfrac{e^{-x}-e^{-2x}}{x}\,dx.$ (Hint. Double integral?)
Question 8[6+2 pts]

Let $S$ be the sphere centered at $(x,y,z)=(0,0,1)$ with radius $1$. Consider the solid $E$ that is inside the sphere $S$ and bounded from above by the cone $z=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$.

  1. Find an iterated integral using the spherical coordinate that calculates the volume of the solid $E$.
  2. Evaluate the integral in (i).
Question 9[8 pts]

Consider the integral sum $$\int_1^2\!\!\int_{1/y}^{y}(x^2+y^2)\,dx\,dy+\int_2^4\!\!\int_{y/4}^{4/y}(x^2+y^2)\,dx\,dy.$$ Rewrite the integral over a region in the $uv$-plane by using the transformation $x=u/v,\;y=uv$. DO NOT evaluate the integral.

Question 10[4+4+4 pts]

Consider the vector field $$\mathbf{F}(x,y,z)=\left\langle e^{x}\cos y+\ln z,\;-e^{ax}\sin y,\;\frac{bx}{z}\right\rangle$$ on the domain $D=\{(x,y,z)\mid z>0\}$, where $a$ and $b$ are two constants. Suppose that $\mathbf{F}$ is conservative on $D$.

  1. Determine the values of $a$ and $b$.
  2. Find a potential function of $\mathbf{F}$.
  3. If $\mathbf{F}$ is a force field, find the work done by $\mathbf{F}$ on the particle that moves from the point $(2,\pi,1)$ to $(0,3,2)$ along the line segment joining them.
Question 11[8 pts]

Find the circulation and flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x,y)=\langle x^2,y^2\rangle$ around and across the closed semicircular path that consists of the upper half semicircle from $(a,0)$ to $(-a,0)$ followed by the line segment on the $x$-axis from $(-a,0)$ to $(a,0)$.

Question 12[8 pts]

Consider the vector field $\mathbf{F}=\operatorname{curl}\mathbf{A}=\nabla\times\mathbf{A}$, where $$\mathbf{A}=\mathbf{A}(x,y,z)=\left\langle y+\sqrt{z},\;e^{xyz},\;\cos(xz)\right\rangle.$$ Compute the outward (upward) flux of $\mathbf{F}$ through the hemisphere $$x^2+y^2+z^2=1,\;z\ge 0.$$

Question 13[8 pts]

Consider the vector field $$\mathbf{F}(x,y,z)=\left\langle 2x^3+y^3,\;y^3+z^3,\;3y^2 z\right\rangle.$$ Compute the outward flux of $\mathbf{F}$ through the surface formed by the paraboloid $z=1-x^2-y^2,\;z\ge 0$, and the disk $x^2+y^2\le 1,\;z=0$.

Question 14[Bonus, 6 pts]

Let $D$ be an open region in the $xy$-plane and let $f$ be a function continuous on $D$. Suppose that the double integral of $f$ over any rectangular subregion $R\subset D$ is zero, i.e., $$\iint_R f(x,y)\,dA=0.$$ Prove that $f(x,y)=0$ for all $(x,y)\in D$.

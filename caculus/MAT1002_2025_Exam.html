Question 1[27 pts]Short questions — no intermediate step required.
  1. True (T) or False (F): The 3D space without the origin is a simply connected region.
  2. True (T) or False (F): Suppose the function $z=f(x,y)$ is continuous with respect to $x$ for any fixed $y$, and continuous with respect to $y$ for any fixed $x$, then it is a continuous function.
  3. True (T) or False (F): The vector field $\mathbf{F}=\left(\dfrac{x}{x^2+y^2},\dfrac{y}{x^2+y^2}\right)$ passes the component test, thus it is conservative in its domain.
  4. True (T) or False (F): Suppose $z=f(x,y)$ is continuous function on the region $R$ where $R=R_1\cup R_2$. Then we have $$\iint_R f(x,y)\,dA=\iint_{R_1} f(x,y)\,dA+\iint_{R_2} f(x,y)\,dA.$$
  5. Rewrite the following integral in Cylindrical coordinates, in the order of $dz\,d\theta\,dr$: $$\int_{-1}^1\!\!\int_0^{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\!\!\int_0^{x^2+y^2} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dy\,dx.$$
  6. Find the directional derivative for the function $w=xy+\cos z-\dfrac{y}{x}$ at the point $(2,1,0)$ in the direction $\left(\tfrac{1}{3},\tfrac{2}{3},\tfrac{2}{3}\right)$.
  7. Find the direction (the unit vector) that is parallel to both the plane $3x-2y+z=0$ and $x+y-3z=0$.
  8. Find the quadratic approximation at $(0,0)$ of $z=\ln(1+x)\sin(y)$.
  9. Compute $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{2n+1}{n!}=(\;\;)$.
Question 2[15 pts]Determine the following limits. If the limit exists (as a real number), find the value and show your steps; otherwise, explain why it does not exist.
  1. $\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\dfrac{\sin(xy)}{x^2+y^2}.$
  2. $\displaystyle\lim_{(x,y)\to(0,0)}\dfrac{x^2 y}{x^4+y^2}.$
  3. $\displaystyle\lim_{(r,\theta)\to(0,\pi/2)}\dfrac{r\cos\theta}{r^2+1}.$
Question 3[6 pts]

If $f(x,y)=x\sin y+y\ln x$, find the second-order derivatives: $$\dfrac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2},\quad\dfrac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y\,\partial x},\quad\dfrac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2},\quad\dfrac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x\,\partial y}.$$

Question 4[6 pts]

Let surface $S$ be characterized by the equation $z=x^2-y^2$. Find the tangent plane $M$ and normal line $L$ to the surface at point $(2,1,3)$.

Question 5[6 pts]

Find the point on the intersection line between plane $2x+y-z=4$ and plane $x+y+z=3$ that has minimum distance from the origin $(0,0,0)$.

Question 6[8 pts]

Find and classify the critical points (as local maxima, local minima, or saddle points) of the function: $$f(x,y)=x^3+y^3-3xy.$$

Question 7[6+6 pts]Evaluate the following integrals.
  1. $\displaystyle\iint_R (3-x-y)\,dA$, where $R$ is the region enclosed by the parabola $y=x^2$ and the line $y=x+2$.
  2. $\displaystyle\int_0^3\!\!\int_{\sqrt{x/3}}^{1} e^{y^3}\,dy\,dx.$
Question 8[4+4 pts]

Consider a solid bounded from below by a sphere of radius one centered at the origin and from above by the paraboloid $z=1-x^2-y^2$.

  1. Express the volume of the solid in terms of a triple integral in cylindrical coordinates.
  2. Find the volume of the solid by evaluating the integral in (a).
Question 9[3+5 pts]

Consider the integral $$\int_0^{2/3}\!\!\int_y^{2(1-y)}(x+2y)e^{y-x}\,dx\,dy.$$

  1. Sketch the integration region $R$ in the $xy$-plane.
  2. Using the transformation $u=x+2y,\;v=x-y$, express it as an integral over a suitable region $G$ in the $uv$-plane, and sketch the region $G$. (DO NOT evaluate the integral.)
Question 10[4+4+4 pts]
  1. For what values of $b$ and $c$ will $$\mathbf{F}=(y^2+2cxz)\,\mathbf{i}+y(bx+cz)\,\mathbf{j}+(y^2+cx^2)\,\mathbf{k}$$ be a gradient field?
  2. Find a potential function of $$\mathbf{F}=(y^2+4xz)\,\mathbf{i}+(2xy+2yz)\,\mathbf{j}+(y^2+2x^2)\,\mathbf{k}.$$
  3. If $\mathbf{F}$ in (b) is a force field, find the work done by $\mathbf{F}$ on the particle that moves from the point $(0,0,0)$ to $(1,2,2)$ along the line segment joining them.
Question 11[8 pts]
  1. Find the flux of the field $\mathbf{F}(x,y)=\langle x,y\rangle$ across the closed path consisting of:
    1. The right semicircle from $(0,2)$ to $(0,-2)$;
    2. The line segment on the $y$-axis from $(0,-2)$ to $(0,2)$.
  2. Find the circulation of the field $\mathbf{F}(x,y)=\langle -y,x\rangle$ around the closed path consisting of:
    1. The right semicircle from $(0,2)$ to $(0,-2)$ (clockwise);
    2. The line segment on the $y$-axis from $(0,-2)$ to $(0,2)$.
Question 12[8 pts]

Consider the vector field $\mathbf{F}(x,y,z)=\langle x^2 y,\;y^2 z,\;z^2 x\rangle$. Compute the outward flux of $\mathbf{F}$ through the surface of the cube bounded by the planes $x=0,\;x=1,\;y=0,\;y=1,\;z=0$, and $z=1$.

Question 13[8 pts]

Consider the vector field $\mathbf{F}(x,y,z)=\langle y^2,\;z^2,\;x^2\rangle$. Let $S$ be the part of the paraboloid $z=4-x^2-y^2$ that lies above the $xy$-plane, oriented upward. The boundary of $S$ is the curve $C$ (a circle in the $xy$-plane). Compute the surface integral $$\iint_S (\nabla\times\mathbf{F})\cdot \mathbf{n}\,d\sigma$$ using Stokes' Theorem.

## 1. 浮力求未知液体密度 (Buoyancy)

**题目**：A rock suspended by a light string. In air, tension $T=39.2$ N. Totally immersed in water, $T=28.4$ N. Totally immersed in an unknown liquid, $T=18.6$ N. Find the density of the unknown liquid.

### 知识点解析（中文）

浮力题的标准套路：绳的读数 = 重量 − 浮力，即 $T = W - \rho_{\text{fluid}}Vg$。水的浮力让你推出"石头的体积 × 重力加速度"（$Vg$），再用未知液体的浮力反推其密度。用比值法最干净，$g$ 和 $V$ 都消掉。

### Solution

In air, $W = T_{\text{air}} = 39.2$ N (air buoyancy negligible).

In water: $T_{\text{water}} = W - \rho_w V g$
$$\rho_w V g = 39.2 - 28.4 = 10.8\ \text{N}.$$

In the unknown liquid: $T = W - \rho V g$
$$\rho V g = 39.2 - 18.6 = 20.6\ \text{N}.$$

Dividing:
$$\frac{\rho}{\rho_w}=\frac{20.6}{10.8}=1.907
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\rho = 1.907\times 1000 = 1.91\times10^3\ \text{kg/m}^3.$$

**Answer:** $\rho = 1.91\times10^{3}$ kg/m³.

## 2. 浮在油水界面的木块 (Sears C12-P12.33)

**题目**：A cubical block of wood, $10.6$ cm on a side, floats at the interface between oil and water with its lower surface $1.50$ cm below the interface. Oil density $790$ kg/m³. (a) Gauge pressure at upper face; (b) gauge pressure at lower face; (c) mass and density of block.

（图：油面在油水界面上方 $10.0$ cm，即木块顶面（界面以上 $9.1$ cm）之上还有 $0.9$ cm 的油。此值从答案反推，见⚠️。）

### 知识点解析（中文）

立方体边长 $L=0.106$ m，下表面在界面下 $1.50$ cm → 浸水高度 $0.0150$ m，浸油高度 $0.106-0.0150=0.0910$ m。(c) 用**浮力 = 排开的两种液体的重量**直接求质量密度，不需要油面位置。(a)(b) 是静水压强 $p=p_0+\rho g h$：上表面之上只有油（深 $0.9$ cm），下表面之上是"整段油 + 一段水"。

### Solution

Let $L=0.106$ m. Submerged in water: $h_w=0.0150$ m; in oil: $h_o=0.0910$ m.

**(c)** Buoyant force = weight of displaced oil + water:
$$Mg=\rho_w g\,L^2 h_w + \rho_o g\,L^2 h_o = g L^2(\rho_w h_w+\rho_o h_o),$$
$$M = (0.106)^2(1000\times0.0150+790\times0.0910)=0.011236(15+71.89)=0.976\ \text{kg}.$$
$$\rho=\frac{M}{L^3}=\frac{0.976}{0.106^3}=820\ \text{kg/m}^3.$$

**(a)** Gauge pressure at the upper face (only oil above, depth $0.0090$ m):
$$p_{\text{upper}}=\rho_o g\,(0.0090)=790\times9.8\times0.0090=69.6\ \text{Pa}.$$

**(b)** Gauge pressure at the lower face (oil column $0.100$ m down to the interface, then water $0.0150$ m):
$$p_{\text{lower}}=\rho_o g\,(0.100)+\rho_w g\,(0.0150)
=774+147=921\ \text{Pa}.$$

**Answer:** (a) $69.6$ Pa; (b) $921$ Pa; (c) $M=0.976$ kg, $\rho=820$ kg/m³.

## 3. 坝内水管与塞子 (Halliday C14-P2)

**题目**：Fresh water behind a dam has depth $D=12$ m. A horizontal pipe $4.0$ cm in diameter passes through the dam at depth $d=6.0$ m. A plug secures the opening. (a) Frictional force between plug and pipe wall; (b) plug removed, what volume exits in $3.0$ h?

### 知识点解析（中文）

(a) 塞子内外压强差 = 该深度的**表压** $p=\rho g d$（水一侧是大气压 + 水压，空气一侧是大气压，两者一减正好只剩 $\rho g d$）。净力 $=pA$，由摩擦力兜住。(b) 塞子拔掉后是**托里拆利（Torricelli）**：开口在自由液面下 $d$，出流速度 $v=\sqrt{2gd}$，再用 $V=vAt$。注意 (a) 用 $d$ 不是 $D$，因为开口深度是 $d=6.0$ m。

### Solution

Pipe radius $r=0.020$ m, so $A=\pi(0.020)^2=1.257\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}^2$.

**(a)** The net force on the plug is the gauge pressure at depth $d$ times the area, balanced by friction $f$:
$$f = \rho g d\, A = (1000)(9.8)(6.0)(1.257\times10^{-3})=74\ \text{N}.$$

**(b)** Efflux speed (Torricelli):
$$v=\sqrt{2gd}=\sqrt{2(9.8)(6.0)}=10.84\ \text{m/s}.$$
Volume in $t=3.0$ h $=10800$ s:
$$V=vAt=(10.84)(1.257\times10^{-3})(10800)=147\ \text{m}^3\approx1.5\times10^2\ \text{m}^3.$$

**Answer:** (a) $f=74$ N; (b) $V=1.5\times10^{2}$ m³.

## 4. 坝面受力与力矩 (Halliday C14-P3)

**题目**：Water stands at depth $D=30.0$ m behind the vertical upstream face of a dam of width $W=250$ m. (a) Net horizontal force from gauge pressure; (b) net torque about a horizontal line through $O$ (parallel to width); (c) effective moment arm $r_{\text{eff}}=\tau/F$.

### 知识点解析（中文）

水压随深度线性增大 $p(y)=\rho g y$（$y$ 从水面往下量）。坝面是一条竖直带，把它切成无数水平细条：$dF=p(y)\,W\,dy$，对 $y$ 从 $0$ 到 $D$ 积分得总力。力矩同理，但每条力对坝底的力臂是 $(D-y)$，积分出一个 $\tfrac16\rho gWD^3$。**有效力臂 = 力矩 ÷ 力 = $D/3$**——合力的作用点在坝底以上 $D/3$（即水压三角形的重心）。

### Solution

**(a)** Force on a strip at depth $y$: $dF = \rho g y\, W\,dy$.
$$F=\int_0^D \rho g W y\,dy = \rho g W\frac{D^2}{2}
=\frac12(1000)(9.8)(250)(30.0)^2=1.10\times10^9\ \text{N}.$$

**(b)** The strip's lever arm about the bottom is $D-y$:
$$\tau=\int_0^D \rho g W y\,(D-y)\,dy
=\rho g W\Big(\frac{D^3}{2}-\frac{D^3}{3}\Big)
=\frac16\rho g W D^3
=1.10\times10^{10}\ \text{N}\cdot\text{m}.$$

**(c)**
$$r_{\text{eff}}=\frac{\tau}{F}=\frac{\tfrac16\rho gWD^3}{\tfrac12\rho gWD^2}=\frac{D}{3}=10\ \text{m}.$$

**Answer:** (a) $F=1.10\times10^{9}$ N; (b) $\tau=1.10\times10^{10}$ N·m; (c) $r_{\text{eff}}=10$ m.

## 5. 大气压随高度的变化 (Barometric formula)

**题目**：Air is an ideal gas with molar mass $M_m$, temperature constant $T$, gravity $g$, sea-level pressure $p_0$. (a) Derive $dp/dy = -(M_m g/RT)\,p$; (b) solve for $p(y)$.

### 知识点解析（中文）

取一小薄层空气（厚 $dy$），压强随高度下降：$dp=-\rho_{\text{air}}g\,dy$。难点在把空气密度 $\rho$ 换成压强 $p$——用理想气体方程 $pV=nRT$，$n/V=p/RT$，而 $\rho=m/V=nM_m/V$，所以 $\rho=M_m p/RT$。代入就是(a)。(b) 分离变量积分，得到指数衰减（barometric formula）。

### Solution

**(a)** Hydrostatic balance of a layer $dy$: $dp=-\rho\,g\,dy$. From $pV=nRT$:
$$\frac{n}{V}=\frac{p}{RT}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \rho=\frac{m}{V}=\frac{nM_m}{V}=\frac{M_m p}{RT}.$$
$$\therefore\quad \frac{dp}{dy}=-\frac{M_m g}{RT}\,p.$$

**(b)** Separate and integrate from $p_0$ at $y=0$:
$$\frac{dp}{p}=-\frac{M_m g}{RT}\,dy
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\ln\frac{p}{p_0}=-\frac{M_m g}{RT}\,y,$$
$$p(y)=p_0\exp\!\Big(-\frac{M_m g}{RT}\,y\Big).$$

**Answer:** (a) $dp/dy=-(M_m g/RT)\,p$; (b) $p(y)=p_0 e^{-M_m g y/RT}$.

## 6. 喷泉出流 (Fluid Flow)

**题目**：Water runs into a fountain, filling all the pipes, at a steady rate $Q=0.750$ m³/s. (a) How fast does it shoot out of a hole $4.50$ cm in diameter? (b) What speed if the hole diameter is three times as large?

### 知识点解析（中文）

纯**连续性方程**（continuity equation）：流量 $Q=Av$。流量恒定，截面积越大速度越小。直径变 3 倍 → 面积变 9 倍 → 速度变 1/9。（答案里的 472 m/s 大得离谱，但就是题目给的 0.750 m³/s 流量硬怼一个 4.5 cm 小孔的结果。）

### Solution

**(a)** $A=\pi r^2=\pi(0.0225)^2=1.59\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}^2$:
$$v_1=\frac{Q}{A}=\frac{0.750}{1.59\times10^{-3}}=472\ \text{m/s}.$$

**(b)** Diameter ×3 → area ×9:
$$v_2=\frac{v_1}{9}=52.4\ \text{m/s}.$$

**Answer:** (a) $472$ m/s; (b) $52.4$ m/s.

## 7. 水平变径管出流 (Halliday C14-P57)

**题目**：Water flows through a horizontal pipe and exits into the atmosphere at speed $v_1=23.0$ m/s. The left and right sections have diameters $5.00$ cm and $3.00$ cm. (a) Volume exiting in $20.0$ min; (b) speed $v_2$ in the left section; (c) gauge pressure in the left section.

### 知识点解析（中文）

右段（细，$3.00$ cm）通大气、速度 $v_1=23.0$ m/s。(a) 流量 $Q=v_1 A_{\text{right}}$，乘时间得体积。(b) 连续性：$v_2=Q/A_{\text{left}}$（细段快、粗段慢）。(c) 水平管用**伯努利（Bernoulli）** $p_2+\tfrac12\rho v_2^2=p_{\text{atm}}+\tfrac12\rho v_1^2$，表压 $p_2-p_{\text{atm}}=\tfrac12\rho(v_1^2-v_2^2)$。

### Solution

Right (exit) area $A_1=\pi(0.015)^2=7.069\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2$; left area $A_2=\pi(0.025)^2=1.963\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}^2$.

**(a)** Flow rate:
$$Q=v_1 A_1=(23.0)(7.069\times10^{-4})=0.01626\ \text{m}^3/\text{s}.$$
$$V=Q\,t=(0.01626)(20\times60)=19.5\ \text{m}^3.$$

**(b)** Continuity $Q=v_2 A_2$:
$$v_2=\frac{Q}{A_2}=\frac{0.01626}{1.963\times10^{-3}}=8.28\ \text{m/s}.$$

**(c)** Bernoulli (horizontal pipe, same height):
$$p_2-p_{\text{atm}}=\tfrac12\rho(v_1^2-v_2^2)
=\tfrac12(1000)(23.0^2-8.28^2)=2.30\times10^5\ \text{Pa}.$$

**Answer:** (a) $19.5$ m³; (b) $8.28$ m/s; (c) $2.30\times10^{5}$ Pa.

## 8. 皮托管测空速 (Halliday C14-P48, Pitot tube)

**题目**：A pitot tube measures the airspeed of an airplane. (a) Use Bernoulli to show $v=\sqrt{2\rho g h/\rho_{\text{air}}}$, where $\rho$ is the U-tube liquid density and $h$ the level difference. (b) If $h=20.0$ cm, $\rho_{\text{air}}=1.03$ kg/m³, $\rho=810$ kg/m³, find $v$.

### 知识点解析（中文）

皮托管的两个口：前端 $A$ 正对来流，空气在这里**滞止**（$v_A=0$，stagnation point）；侧壁 $B$ 处空气以飞机速度 $v$ 流过。同一高度用伯努利：$p_A+\tfrac12\rho v_A^2=p_B+\tfrac12\rho v^2$，得 $p_A-p_B=\tfrac12\rho_{\text{air}}v^2$（动压）。这个压强差由 U 形管液柱读出：$p_A-p_B=\rho g h$。二者相等解出 $v$。

### Solution

**(a)** At $A$ (stagnation) $v_A=0$; at $B$, $v_B=v$. Bernoulli (same elevation):
$$p_A+\tfrac12\rho_{\text{air}}v_A^2=p_B+\tfrac12\rho_{\text{air}}v_B^2
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
p_A-p_B=\tfrac12\rho_{\text{air}}v^2.$$
The U-tube measures $p_A-p_B=\rho g h$, so
$$\tfrac12\rho_{\text{air}}v^2=\rho g h
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
v=\sqrt{\frac{2\rho g h}{\rho_{\text{air}}}}.$$

**(b)**
$$v=\sqrt{\frac{2(810)(9.8)(0.20)}{1.03}}
=\sqrt{3083}=55.5\ \text{m/s}.$$

**Answer:** (a) $v=\sqrt{2\rho g h/\rho_{\text{air}}}$; (b) $55.5$ m/s.

## 9. 文丘里流量计 (Venturi meter)

**题目**：Derive the flow speed $v_1=\sqrt{2gh\big/\big((A_1/A_2)^2-1\big)}$, where $A_1,A_2$ are the cross-sectional areas and $h$ the difference in the vertical tube levels.

### 知识点解析（中文）

文丘里管：宽段（面积 $A_1$、速度 $v_1$、压强 $p_1$）→ 窄喉（面积 $A_2$、速度 $v_2$、压强 $p_2$）。两条方程：**连续性** $A_1v_1=A_2v_2$ 把 $v_2$ 用 $v_1$ 表示；**伯努利**（水平）$p_1+\tfrac12\rho v_1^2=p_2+\tfrac12\rho v_2^2$ 把压强差写成速度差。压强差又由竖直管液柱读出 $p_1-p_2=\rho g h$。代入消元即得。

### Solution

Continuity: $A_1v_1=A_2v_2 \Rightarrow v_2=(A_1/A_2)v_1$.

Bernoulli (horizontal): $p_1+\tfrac12\rho v_1^2=p_2+\tfrac12\rho v_2^2$:
$$p_1-p_2=\tfrac12\rho(v_2^2-v_1^2)
=\tfrac12\rho v_1^2\Big[\Big(\tfrac{A_1}{A_2}\Big)^2-1\Big].$$

The vertical tubes give $p_1-p_2=\rho g h$, so
$$\rho g h=\tfrac12\rho v_1^2\Big[\Big(\tfrac{A_1}{A_2}\Big)^2-1\Big]
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
v_1=\sqrt{\frac{2gh}{(A_1/A_2)^2-1}}.$$

**Answer:** $v_1=\sqrt{2gh\big/((A_1/A_2)^2-1)}$.

## 10. 大桶排水 (Draining bucket)

**题目**：A bucket of height $H$, cross-sectional area $A_1$, open to atmosphere, has a small faucet of area $A_2\ll A_1$ at the bottom. (a) Show the efflux speed is $\approx\sqrt{2gh}$; (b) show $dh/dt=-(A_2/A_1)\sqrt{2gh}$; (c) find $h(t)$ with $h=H$ at $t=0$; (d) total drain time for $H=2.00$ m, $A_1=0.800$ m², $A_2=1.00\times10^{-4}A_1$.

### 知识点解析（中文）

(a) 托里拆利：桶顶和出水口都通大气，伯努利直接给 $v=\sqrt{2gh}$（顶面速度因 $A_2\ll A_1$ 忽略）。(b) 体积守恒：$A_1\,dh/dt$（桶内水体积下降率）= $-A_2 v$（流出体积率）。(c) 分离变量 $\int dh/\sqrt h$ 得 $\sqrt h$ 随时间线性下降。(d) 令 $h=0$ 解出排水时间 $t=(A_1/A_2)\sqrt{2H/g}$。⚠️ 数值见下。

### Solution

**(a)** Bernoulli between the top surface (pressure $p_{\text{atm}}$, speed $\approx0$, height $h$) and the faucet (pressure $p_{\text{atm}}$, height $0$, speed $v$):
$$p_{\text{atm}}+\rho g h=p_{\text{atm}}+\tfrac12\rho v^2
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
v=\sqrt{2gh}.$$

**(b)** Volume conservation:
$$A_1\frac{dh}{dt}=-A_2 v=-A_2\sqrt{2gh}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\frac{dh}{dt}=-\frac{A_2}{A_1}\sqrt{2gh}.$$

**(c)** Separate and integrate:
$$\frac{dh}{\sqrt h}=-\frac{A_2}{A_1}\sqrt{2g}\,dt
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
2\sqrt h = 2\sqrt H-\frac{A_2}{A_1}\sqrt{2g}\,t,$$
$$h(t)=\Big(\sqrt H-\frac{A_2}{A_1}\sqrt{\tfrac g2}\,t\Big)^2.$$

**(d)** Set $h=0$:
$$t=\frac{A_1}{A_2}\sqrt{\frac{2H}{g}}
=\frac{A_1}{1.00\times10^{-4}A_1}\sqrt{\frac{2(2.00)}{9.8}}
=\frac{1}{1.00\times10^{-4}}\times0.639
=6.39\times10^3\ \text{s}.$$

**Answer:** (a) $v=\sqrt{2gh}$; (b) $dh/dt=-(A_2/A_1)\sqrt{2gh}$; (c) $h=\big(\sqrt H-\tfrac{A_2}{A_1}\sqrt{g/2}\,t\big)^2$; (d) $t=6.39\times10^{3}$ s.

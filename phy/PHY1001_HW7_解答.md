## 1. 非均匀杆的质心 (Halliday C12-P10)

**题目**：A nonuniform bar of length $L=9.50$ m is suspended at rest horizontally by two massless cords. The left cord makes $\theta=30.0^\circ$ with the vertical; the right cord makes $\phi=60.0^\circ$ with the vertical. Compute the distance $x$ from the left end to the center of mass.

### 知识点解析（中文）

平衡 = 合力为零 + 合力矩为零（对任意点取矩都成立）。杆水平静止，两条绳竖直分量之和托住重量，水平分量互相抵消。**取矩技巧**：对左端取矩时，左绳的力臂为零，只有重量 $W$（作用在质心 $x$ 处）和右绳竖直分量（作用在右端 $L$ 处）有矩；对右端取矩同理。两条力矩方程 + 一条水平平衡，联立即可解出 $x$，重量 $W$ 自动消掉。

### Solution

Let $T_1$ = tension in the left cord, $T_2$ = tension in the right cord, $W$ = weight of the bar.

**Vertical equilibrium:**
$$T_1\cos\theta + T_2\cos\phi = W.$$

**Horizontal equilibrium:** the horizontal components cancel,
$$T_1\sin\theta = T_2\sin\phi.$$

**Torque about the left end** (the left cord and its vertical/horizontal components have zero lever arm there; the right cord's horizontal component passes through the bar's level, so only its vertical component has a moment arm):
$$Wx = T_2\cos\phi\cdot L
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
T_2 = \frac{Wx}{L\cos\phi}.$$

**Torque about the right end:**
$$W(L-x) = T_1\cos\theta\cdot L
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
T_1 = \frac{W(L-x)}{L\cos\theta}.$$

Substitute $T_1,T_2$ into the horizontal equation:
$$\frac{W(L-x)}{L\cos\theta}\sin\theta = \frac{Wx}{L\cos\phi}\sin\phi
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
(L-x)\tan\theta = x\tan\phi,$$
$$x = \frac{L\tan\theta}{\tan\theta+\tan\phi}
= \frac{9.50\times\tan 30^\circ}{\tan 30^\circ+\tan 60^\circ}
= \frac{9.50\times 0.5774}{0.5774+1.7321}
= 2.38\ \text{m}.$$

**Answer:** $x = 2.38$ m.

## 2. 推轮子过台阶 (Halliday C12-P17)

**题目**：A wheel of radius $r=8.00$ cm and mass $m=0.600$ kg. What constant horizontal force $F$ applied at the axle is needed to just raise the wheel over a step of height $h=3.00$ cm?

### 知识点解析（中文）

轮子刚要被抬起的一瞬间，地面对它的支持力 $N=0$，轮子只绕**台阶棱角 P** 转动（接触点从地面移到台阶角上）。这时对 P 取矩：重力的力臂是质心到 P 的水平距离 $d=\sqrt{r^2-(r-h)^2}=\sqrt{2rh-h^2}$（勾股定理，因为质心到 P 的连线就是半径 $r$），外力 $F$ 的力臂是质心到 P 的竖直距离 $r-h$。临界时二力矩相等。

### Solution

At the instant the wheel is about to lift off, the normal force from the floor vanishes and the wheel pivots about the step corner $P$.

The distance from the center to $P$ is the radius $r$. The center is vertically above the ground by $r$, and $P$ is a height $h$ above the ground, so the **vertical** drop from center to $P$ is $r-h$. The **horizontal** distance from $P$ to the vertical line through the center is therefore
$$d=\sqrt{r^2-(r-h)^2}=\sqrt{2rh-h^2}.$$

**Torque about P** (net torque = 0 at the threshold):
- weight $mg$ has lever arm $d$;
- applied force $F$ (horizontal, through the axle) has lever arm $r-h$.

$$F(r-h)=mg\,d
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
F=\frac{mg\sqrt{2rh-h^2}}{r-h}.$$

$$d=\sqrt{2(0.080)(0.030)-(0.030)^2}=\sqrt{0.0048-0.0009}=\sqrt{0.0039}=0.06245\ \text{m},$$
$$F=\frac{(0.600)(9.8)(0.06245)}{0.050}
=\frac{0.3672}{0.050}=7.34\ \text{N}.$$

**Answer:** $F = 7.34$ N.

## 3. 推倒柱子 (Knocking Over a Post)

**题目**：A post weighing $W=400$ N and height $h$ rests on a rough horizontal surface with $\mu_s=0.30$. The upper end is held by a rope fastened to the surface, making an angle $36.9^\circ$ with the post. A horizontal force $\vec F$ is applied at the **midpoint** of the post. What is the largest $F$ without causing the post to slip?

（图：绳从柱顶斜向下连到地面，在 $F$ 的**对侧**；$F$ 水平推柱中点。$36.9^\circ$ 是 3-4-5 角：$\sin36.9^\circ=0.6,\ \cos36.9^\circ=0.8$。）

### 知识点解析（中文）

三件事同时发生：① 绳斜向下拉柱顶，水平分量 $T\sin36.9^\circ$ 抵消 $F$、竖直分量 $T\cos36.9^\circ$ 把柱顶往下拽，**增大**了地面的正压力 $N=W+T\cos36.9^\circ$；② 对柱底取矩（柱不翻），$F\cdot\frac h2 = T\sin36.9^\circ\cdot h$，得到 $F$ 与 $T$ 的关系；③ 静摩擦要兜住净水平力，$f=\mu_s N$ 时开始打滑。三式联立求出临界 $F$。关键直觉：绳往下拽得越狠（$T$ 越大），正压力越大、地面抓得越牢，所以柱子是"先被拽稳了"再滑。

### Solution

Let $T$ be the rope tension. The rope pulls the top of the post **down and sideways** toward the anchor, with components
$$T\sin 36.9^\circ \ (\text{horizontal}),\qquad T\cos 36.9^\circ \ (\text{vertical, downward}).$$

**Torque about the bottom end** (the post does not tip):
$$F\cdot\frac{h}{2}=T\sin 36.9^\circ\cdot h
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
F=2T\sin 36.9^\circ = 1.2\,T
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
T=\frac{F}{1.2}.$$

**Horizontal equilibrium** (friction $f$ opposes the net push):
$$F=T\sin 36.9^\circ + f
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
f=F-0.6\,T=F-0.6\!\left(\frac{F}{1.2}\right)=0.5\,F.$$

**Vertical equilibrium:**
$$N=W+T\cos 36.9^\circ
=400+0.8\!\left(\frac{F}{1.2}\right)
=400+\tfrac23 F.$$

**No-slip condition** $f\le \mu_s N$:
$$0.5\,F \le 0.30\!\left(400+\tfrac23 F\right)=120+0.2\,F,$$
$$0.3\,F\le 120
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
F\le 400\ \text{N}.$$

**Answer:** $F = 400$ N.

## 4. 斜面上靠绕线支撑的圆柱

**题目**：A uniform solid cylinder of mass $M$ is supported on a ramp rising at angle $\theta$ above the horizontal by a wire wrapped around its rim, pulling tangentially parallel to the ramp. (a) Show friction must be present for balance. (b) Show the wire tension equals the friction force, and find the tension.

### 知识点解析（中文）

(a) **无摩擦必翻**：取质心为轴，重力、正压力都过质心（力矩为零），只剩线张力 $T$ 作用在轮缘上（力臂 $R$），净力矩 $TR\ne0$，柱子必转，不可能平衡 → 必须有摩擦。(b) 摩擦 $f$ 作用在触地点（也在轮缘上），与 $T$ 对质心的力矩反向抵消 → $T=f$；再沿斜面受力平衡 $T+f=Mg\sin\theta$ → $T=f=\tfrac12 Mg\sin\theta$。

### Solution

**(a)** Choose the center of mass as the axis. Gravity and the normal force both pass through the COM (zero torque). The wire tension $T$ acts tangentially at the rim, a distance $R$ from the COM, giving torque $TR$. With no friction there is no opposing torque, so the net torque is $TR\neq 0$ and the cylinder would rotate — equilibrium is impossible. Hence friction is required.

**(b)** With friction $f$ acting at the contact point (also on the rim, opposite side), the torques about the COM are
$$T R - f R = 0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
T = f.$$
(Equivalently: tension at the top of the rim and friction at the bottom create equal and opposite torques.)

**Force balance along the ramp** (up the ramp: $T$ and $f$; down the ramp: $Mg\sin\theta$):
$$T + f - Mg\sin\theta = 0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
2T = Mg\sin\theta,$$
$$T = f = \tfrac12 Mg\sin\theta.$$

**Answer:** $T = f = \dfrac{Mg\sin\theta}{2}$.

## 5. 登山绳的杨氏模量 (Stress on a Mountaineer's Rope)

**题目**：A nylon rope elongates $1.10$ m under a $65.0$-kg climber. Length $L=45.0$ m, diameter $d=7.0$ mm. Find Young's modulus $Y$.

### 知识点解析（中文）

直接套杨氏模量定义 $Y=\dfrac{F/A}{\Delta L/L}=\dfrac{FL}{A\,\Delta L}$。注意单位：直径 $7.0$ mm → 半径 $3.5\times10^{-3}$ m，横截面积 $A=\pi r^2$；$F=mg$ 是绳的拉伸载荷。

### Solution

$$F=mg=(65.0)(9.8)=637\ \text{N},$$
$$A=\pi r^2=\pi(3.5\times10^{-3})^2=3.848\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^2,$$
$$Y=\frac{F/A}{\Delta L/L}=\frac{FL}{A\,\Delta L}
=\frac{(637)(45.0)}{(3.848\times10^{-5})(1.10)}
=6.77\times10^{8}\ \text{Pa}.$$

**Answer:** $Y = 6.77\times10^{8}$ Pa.

## 6. 理想气体的体积模量 (Bulk Modulus of an Ideal Gas)

**题目**：Equation of state $pV=nRT$. (a) Show that for isothermal compression (constant $T$) the bulk modulus $B=p$. (b) For adiabatic compression ($pV^\gamma=\text{const}$), show $B=\gamma p$.

### 知识点解析（中文）

体积模量定义 $B=-V\dfrac{dp}{dV}$（负号因为压强增、体积减，取正）。核心就是：固定温度或绝热约束，把 $p$ 写成 $V$ 的函数再求导。

### Solution

Bulk modulus:
$$B=-V\frac{dp}{dV}.$$

**(a) Isothermal** ($T$ constant): $pV=nRT=\text{const}$, so $p=\dfrac{nRT}{V}$.
$$\frac{dp}{dV}=-\frac{nRT}{V^2}=-\frac{p}{V}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
B=-V\!\left(-\frac{p}{V}\right)=p.$$

**(b) Adiabatic**: $pV^\gamma=\text{const}$, so $p=C\,V^{-\gamma}$.
$$\frac{dp}{dV}=-\gamma C\,V^{-\gamma-1}=-\gamma\frac{p}{V}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
B=-V\!\left(-\gamma\frac{p}{V}\right)=\gamma p.$$

**Answer:** (a) $B=p$; (b) $B=\gamma p$.

## 7. 下坡徒步的膝软骨剪切 (Downhill Hiking)

**题目**：The force on knee cartilage can be up to 8× body weight. Cartilage area $A=10$ cm², shear modulus $S=12$ MPa. Hiker + pack combined mass $m=110$ kg. The maximum impact force ($8mg$) acts at $12^\circ$ with the cartilage. Through what angle is the cartilage deformed?

### 知识点解析（中文）

剪切应变（shear strain）= 剪切角 $\phi=\dfrac{x}{h}$（弧度）。剪应力只取力的**平行于软骨面**的分量 $F\sin12^\circ$（垂直分量 $F\cos12^\circ$ 是压缩，不引起剪切）。$\text{shear stress}=\dfrac{F\sin12^\circ}{A}$，再除以剪切模量 $S$ 得剪切角，最后弧度转角度。

### Solution

Total impact force:
$$F=8mg=8(110)(9.8)=8624\ \text{N}.$$

The **shear (parallel) component** is $F\sin12^\circ$:
$$F_{\text{shear}}=8624\sin12^\circ=1793\ \text{N}.$$

Area: $A=10\ \text{cm}^2=1.0\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}^2$.

Shear stress:
$$\frac{F_{\text{shear}}}{A}=\frac{1793}{1.0\times10^{-3}}=1.79\times10^{6}\ \text{Pa}.$$

Shear strain (deformation angle in radians):
$$\phi=\frac{x}{h}=\frac{\text{shear stress}}{S}
=\frac{1.79\times10^{6}}{12\times10^{6}}
=0.15\ \text{rad}.$$

In degrees:
$$0.15\times\frac{180^\circ}{\pi}=8.6^\circ.$$

**Answer:** $\phi = 0.15$ rad $= 8.6^\circ$.

1. Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors

Q1
Find all eigenvalues and eigenvectors of $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ -2 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$.
Compute the characteristic polynomial, find eigenvalues, and determine the eigenspace for each eigenvalue.
Step-by-Step Solution
Step 1: Characteristic polynomial. $$p_A(\lambda) = \det(\lambda I - A) = \det\begin{pmatrix} \lambda-3 & -2 \\ 2 & \lambda+2 \end{pmatrix} = (\lambda-3)(\lambda+2) + 4 = \lambda^2 - \lambda - 2 = (\lambda-2)(\lambda+1).$$ Step 2: Eigenvalues. $\lambda_1 = 2,\; \lambda_2 = -1$.

Step 3: Eigenspace for $\lambda_1 = 2$. Solve $(2I - A)v = 0$: $$\begin{pmatrix} -1 & -2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} v_1 \\ v_2 \end{pmatrix} = 0 \implies -v_1 - 2v_2 = 0 \implies v_1 = -2v_2.$$ So $v = t \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix},\; t \in \mathbb{R}$. Eigenspace: $E_2 = \operatorname{span}\left\{\begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\right\}$, $\dim(E_2) = 1$.

Step 4: Eigenspace for $\lambda_2 = -1$. Solve $(-I - A)v = 0$: $$\begin{pmatrix} -4 & -2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} v_1 \\ v_2 \end{pmatrix} = 0 \implies 2v_1 + v_2 = 0 \implies v_2 = -2v_1.$$ So $v = s \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix},\; s \in \mathbb{R}$. Eigenspace: $E_{-1} = \operatorname{span}\left\{\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\right\}$, $\dim(E_{-1}) = 1$.
知识点：特征值与特征向量的求解四步法
① 计算特征多项式 $p_A(\lambda) = \det(\lambda I - A)$
② 求根得到特征值（按重数计）
③ 对每个特征值解齐次方程组 $(\lambda_i I - A)v = 0$ 得特征空间
④ 特征空间的维数 = 几何重数 ≤ 代数重数

验证技巧：检查 trace = λ₁ + λ₂ → 3 + (-2) = 1 = 2 + (-1) ✓；det = λ₁·λ₂ → 3·(-2) - 2·(-2) = -2 = 2·(-1) ✓
Q2
Find the eigenvalues of $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & -3 \\ 0 & 3 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$.
Solution
Since $A$ is upper triangular, eigenvalues are the diagonal entries: $\lambda_1 = 2,\; \lambda_2 = 3,\; \lambda_3 = -1$.
Why? For a triangular matrix, $\det(\lambda I - A) = (\lambda - a_{11})(\lambda - a_{22})\cdots(\lambda - a_{nn})$.
考点：三角矩阵的特征值直接等于对角线元素。这是考试重要快捷技巧——看到三角矩阵不需要展开行列式。上三角/下三角都是如此。
Q3
Find the eigenvalues of $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$.
Solution
Method 1 — Direct computation: $$\det(\lambda I - A) = \det\begin{pmatrix} \lambda-2 & -1 & -1 \\ -1 & \lambda-2 & -1 \\ -1 & -1 & \lambda-2 \end{pmatrix}.$$ Subtract row 1 from rows 2 and 3, then expand: $p_A(\lambda) = (\lambda-4)(\lambda-1)^2$.

Method 2 — Structure recognition: $A = I + J$ where $J$ is the all-ones matrix. $J$ has eigenvalues $3, 0, 0$. So $A = I + J$ has eigenvalues $1+3=4$ and $1+0=1$ (multiplicity 2).
Eigenvalues: $\lambda = 4$ (simple), $\lambda = 1$ (double).

Eigenvectors: For $\lambda=4$, $v = (1,1,1)^\top$. For $\lambda=1$, any vector orthogonal to $(1,1,1)^\top$, e.g., $(1,-1,0)^\top$ and $(1,1,-2)^\top$.
考点：结构识别（Structure Recognition）。$A = I + J$ 这类分解在考试中反复出现。全1矩阵$J$的特征值：一个$n$（对应特征向量$(1,1,...,1)^\top$），其余全是0。加上$cI$后特征值都加$c$。
Q4
Complex eigenvalues: $A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. Find eigenvalues.
Solution
$$\det(\lambda I - A) = \det\begin{pmatrix} \lambda & -1 \\ 1 & \lambda \end{pmatrix} = \lambda^2 + 1.$$ Eigenvalues: $\lambda = i$ and $\lambda = -i$ (purely imaginary).
This shows that real matrices can have complex eigenvalues. The complex eigenvalues come in conjugate pairs.
考点：实矩阵可以有复特征值，且复特征值共轭成对出现。由代数基本定理，$n \times n$ 矩阵在 $\mathbb{C}$ 中恰好有 $n$ 个特征值（按重数计）。

2. Diagonalization & the Spectral Theorem

Q5
Is $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ diagonalizable? Justify.
Solution
No. $A$ is upper triangular, so eigenvalue $\lambda = 1$ (algebraic multiplicity 2).
Eigenspace: solve $(I - A)v = 0$: $$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} v_1 \\ v_2 \end{pmatrix} = 0 \implies v_2 = 0.$$ So $E_1 = \operatorname{span}\left\{\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\right\}$, $\dim(E_1) = 1$.
Geometric multiplicity (1) < algebraic multiplicity (2) → not diagonalizable.
This is a classic Jordan block.
考点：可对角化的充要条件。每个特征值的几何重数 = 代数重数。若存在任一特征值的几何重数 < 代数重数，则矩阵不可对角化（亏损矩阵 / defective matrix）。$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ 是最经典的反例——Jordan块。
Q6
Diagonalize $A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$.
Solution
Step 1: $\det(\lambda I - A) = \lambda^2 - 1 = (\lambda-1)(\lambda+1)$. Eigenvalues: $\lambda_1 = 1,\; \lambda_2 = -1$.
Step 2: For $\lambda = 1$: $(I - A)v = 0 \implies v = t(1,1)^\top$.
For $\lambda = -1$: $(-I - A)v = 0 \implies v = s(1,-1)^\top$.
Step 3: Two linearly independent eigenvectors → diagonalizable. $$P = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}, \quad D = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}, \quad A = P D P^{-1}.$$
对角化步骤：①求特征值 ②求特征向量 ③以特征向量为列组成$P$，以特征值为对角线组成$D$，则 $A = PDP^{-1}$。
注意：$P$中特征向量的顺序必须与$D$中特征值的顺序对应。
Q7
State the Spectral Theorem for real symmetric matrices.
Solution
Spectral Theorem: If $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ is symmetric ($A^\top = A$), then:
  1. All eigenvalues of $A$ are real.
  2. $A$ is orthogonally diagonalizable: there exists an orthogonal matrix $Q$ ($Q^\top Q = I$) and a diagonal matrix $\Lambda$ such that $A = Q\Lambda Q^\top$.
  3. Equivalently, $\mathbb{R}^n$ has an orthonormal basis consisting of eigenvectors of $A$.
Key consequence: Eigenvectors corresponding to distinct eigenvalues of a symmetric matrix are orthogonal.
谱定理（实对称矩阵）：对称矩阵的特征值全是实数，且可正交对角化 $A = Q\Lambda Q^\top$。这意味着存在由特征向量组成的$\mathbb{R}^n$的标准正交基。这是线性代数中最重要的定理之一。
📚 Diagonalization — Equivalent Conditions

For $A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}$, the following are equivalent:

  1. $A$ is diagonalizable ($A = PDP^{-1}$ with $D$ diagonal).
  2. $A$ has $n$ linearly independent eigenvectors (an eigenbasis).
  3. For every eigenvalue, geometric multiplicity = algebraic multiplicity.

Sufficient condition: If $A$ has $n$ distinct eigenvalues, then $A$ is diagonalizable. (Not necessary — a matrix with repeated eigenvalues can still be diagonalizable if all geometric multiplicities equal algebraic multiplicities.)

3. Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)

Q8
Compute the full SVD of $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$.
Solution
Step 1: Compute $A^\top A$. $$A^\top A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 \end{pmatrix}.$$ Step 2: Eigenvalues of $A^\top A$ → singular values. $\det(\lambda I - A^\top A) = (\lambda-2)^2 - 4 = \lambda^2 - 4\lambda = \lambda(\lambda-4)$. Eigenvalues: $\lambda_1 = 4,\; \lambda_2 = 0$.
Singular values: $\sigma_1 = \sqrt{4} = 2,\; \sigma_2 = 0$. Rank $r = 1$.

Step 3: Right singular vectors (eigenvectors of $A^\top A$).
For $\lambda = 4$: $v_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,1)^\top$.
For $\lambda = 0$: $v_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,-1)^\top$.

Step 4: Left singular vectors. $u_1 = \frac{1}{\sigma_1} A v_1 = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}$.
Extend to orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^3$: $u_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,-1,0)^\top$, $u_3 = (0,0,1)^\top$.

Step 5: Assemble. $$A = U\Sigma V^\top = \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} 1/\sqrt{2} & 1/\sqrt{2} & 0 \\ 1/\sqrt{2} & -1/\sqrt{2} & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}}_{U} \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}}_{\Sigma} \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} 1/\sqrt{2} & 1/\sqrt{2} \\ 1/\sqrt{2} & -1/\sqrt{2} \end{pmatrix}^\top}_{V^\top}.$$ Compact SVD: $A = \sigma_1 u_1 v_1^\top = 2 \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,1) = \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$.
SVD 计算五步法：
① 计算 $A^\top A$（对称半正定）
② 求 $A^\top A$ 的特征值 $\lambda_i$，奇异值 $\sigma_i = \sqrt{\lambda_i}$（非零的有 $r$ 个）
③ 求 $A^\top A$ 的标准正交特征向量 = 右奇异向量 $v_i$
④ $u_i = \frac{1}{\sigma_i} A v_i$（$i = 1,...,r$），再扩充为 $\mathbb{R}^m$ 的标准正交基
⑤ 组装 $U, \Sigma, V$

简洁SVD: $A = \sum_{i=1}^r \sigma_i u_i v_i^\top$（秩-1矩阵之和）
Q9
From SVD, identify the four fundamental subspaces.
Solution
Given full SVD $A = U\Sigma V^\top$ where $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $\text{rank}(A) = r$:
SubspaceBasis from SVDDimensionLives in
Column space $C(A)$$u_1,\dots,u_r$$r$$\mathbb{R}^m$
Nullspace $N(A)$$v_{r+1},\dots,v_n$$n-r$$\mathbb{R}^n$
Row space $\text{Row}(A)$$v_1,\dots,v_r$$r$$\mathbb{R}^n$
Left nullspace $N(A^\top)$$u_{r+1},\dots,u_m$$m-r$$\mathbb{R}^m$
Orthogonal complement relations: $\text{Row}(A) \perp N(A)$, $C(A) \perp N(A^\top)$.
SVD 最强大的性质之一：直接给出四个基本子空间的标准正交基。记住：$V$ 的前 $r$ 列是行空间，后 $n-r$ 列是零空间；$U$ 的前 $r$ 列是列空间，后 $m-r$ 列是左零空间。
📚 SVD Key Facts
  • Right singular vectors $v_i$ are eigenvectors of $A^\top A$: $A^\top A v_i = \sigma_i^2 v_i$
  • Left singular vectors $u_i$ are eigenvectors of $AA^\top$: $AA^\top u_i = \sigma_i^2 u_i$
  • $A^\top A$ and $AA^\top$ share the same nonzero eigenvalues
  • Number of nonzero singular values = $\text{rank}(A)$
  • Frobenius norm from SVD: $\|A\|_F = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^r \sigma_i^2}$
  • Compact SVD: $A = \sum_{i=1}^r \sigma_i u_i v_i^\top$ (sum of rank-1 matrices)

4. Least Squares Problems

Q10
Solve the least squares problem: $\min_{\mathbf{x}} \|A\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{b}\|$ with $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $\mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$.
Solution
Step 1: Form the normal equation. $$A^\top A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}, \quad A^\top \mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}.$$ Normal equation: $A^\top A \hat{\mathbf{x}} = A^\top \mathbf{b}$: $$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}.$$ Step 2: Solve. From row 1: $2x_1 + x_2 = 5$. From row 2: $x_1 + 2x_2 = 6$.
Solving: $x_1 = \frac{4}{3}$, $x_2 = \frac{7}{3}$.
The LS solution is $\hat{\mathbf{x}} = (\frac{4}{3}, \frac{7}{3})^\top$.
Verification: Residual $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{b} - A\hat{\mathbf{x}} = (1,2,4)^\top - (\frac{4}{3}, \frac{7}{3}, \frac{11}{3})^\top = (-\frac{1}{3}, -\frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{3})^\top$. Check $\mathbf{r} \perp C(A)$: $A^\top \mathbf{r} = (0,0)^\top$ ✓.
最小二乘解题步骤：①计算 $A^\top A$ 和 $A^\top \mathbf{b}$ ②解正规方程 $A^\top A \hat{\mathbf{x}} = A^\top \mathbf{b}$
几何意义：$A\hat{\mathbf{x}}$ 是 $\mathbf{b}$ 在列空间 $C(A)$ 上的正交投影，残差 $\mathbf{b} - A\hat{\mathbf{x}}$ ⟂ $C(A)$。
满列秩时：$\hat{\mathbf{x}} = (A^\top A)^{-1} A^\top \mathbf{b}$（唯一解）
Q11
Prove that the normal equation $A^\top A\mathbf{y} = A^\top\mathbf{b}$ always has at least one solution.
Proof
We need to show $A^\top \mathbf{b} \in C(A^\top A)$ for any $\mathbf{b}$.

Recall Lemma 17.0: $N(A^\top A) = N(A)$. Taking orthogonal complements: $$\text{Row}(A^\top A) = N(A^\top A)^\perp = N(A)^\perp = \text{Row}(A) = C(A^\top).$$ Since $C(A^\top A) = \text{Row}(A^\top A)$ (for symmetric $A^\top A$) and $A^\top \mathbf{b} \in C(A^\top) = C(A^\top A)$, the normal equation is always consistent. ∎
利用 $N(A^\top A) = N(A)$ 取正交补：行空间相等，因此 $A^\top \mathbf{b} \in C(A^\top) = C(A^\top A)$。正规方程对任意 $\mathbf{b}$ 总有解，但解不一定唯一（除非 $A$ 列满秩）。
📚 Least Squares — Three Equivalent Characterizations

For $\min_{\mathbf{x}} \|A\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{b}\|$, the following are equivalent:

  1. $\mathbf{y}$ minimizes $\|A\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{b}\|$
  2. $\mathbf{b} - A\mathbf{y} \perp C(A)$ (residual ⟂ column space)
  3. $A^\top A\mathbf{y} = A^\top \mathbf{b}$ (Normal Equation)

5. Quadratic Forms & Positive Definiteness

Q12
Classify $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ 0 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$ as PD, PSD, I, NSD, or ND.
Solution
Eigenvalues: $\lambda_1 = 3 > 0$, $\lambda_2 = -2 < 0$. Signs are mixed → Indefinite (I).
For $\mathbf{x} = (1,0)^\top$: $\mathbf{x}^\top A\mathbf{x} = 3 > 0$. For $\mathbf{x} = (0,1)^\top$: $\mathbf{x}^\top A\mathbf{x} = -2 < 0$.
判定方法：看特征值的符号。
全部 > 0 → PD（正定）；全部 ≥ 0 → PSD（半正定）；全部 < 0 → ND（负定）；全部 ≤ 0 → NSD（半负定）；有正有负 → I（不定）。
Q13
Prove: If $A$ is positive definite, then all eigenvalues of $A$ are strictly positive.
Proof
Let $\lambda$ be an eigenvalue of $A$ with eigenvector $\mathbf{v} \neq \mathbf{0}$. Then $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$.
Multiply by $\mathbf{v}^\top$: $\mathbf{v}^\top A\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}^\top \mathbf{v} = \lambda \|\mathbf{v}\|^2$.
Since $A$ is PD, $\mathbf{v}^\top A\mathbf{v} > 0$ for $\mathbf{v} \neq \mathbf{0}$. And $\|\mathbf{v}\|^2 > 0$.
Therefore $\lambda = \frac{\mathbf{v}^\top A\mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{v}\|^2} > 0$. ∎
正定矩阵的三个等价条件：① $A$ 正定（$\mathbf{x}^\top A\mathbf{x} > 0$ 对所有 $\mathbf{x} \neq 0$）② 所有特征值 > 0 ③ $A = R^\top R$，其中 $R$ 可逆（Cholesky分解）。这三者等价，在证明题中经常互相转化使用。
Q14
Rewrite $a x^2 + b x y + c y^2 = \alpha$ using a symmetric matrix, and give conditions for an ellipse vs. hyperbola.
Solution
Let $\mathbf{u} = (x, y)^\top$. The equation is $\mathbf{u}^\top A \mathbf{u} = \alpha$ where $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b/2 \\ b/2 & c \end{pmatrix}$ (symmetric).

Diagonalize $A = Q\Lambda Q^\top$ with $\Lambda = \text{diag}(\lambda_1, \lambda_2)$. Let $\tilde{\mathbf{u}} = Q^\top\mathbf{u} = (\tilde{x}, \tilde{y})^\top$. Then: $$\lambda_1 \tilde{x}^2 + \lambda_2 \tilde{y}^2 = \alpha.$$ Ellipse: $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ have the same sign and $\alpha$ shares that sign. Then $\frac{\tilde{x}^2}{\alpha/\lambda_1} + \frac{\tilde{y}^2}{\alpha/\lambda_2} = 1$.
Hyperbola: $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ have opposite signs. Then one squared term is positive, the other negative.
Parabola: one eigenvalue = 0 (degenerate case, $A$ singular).
二次曲线分类：椭圆（特征值同号）、双曲线（特征值异号）、抛物线（一个特征值为零）。这是特征值几何应用的重要例子——通过正交对角化旋转坐标系，将一般二次方程化为标准型。

6. Gram-Schmidt Orthogonalization

Q15
Apply Gram-Schmidt to $\mathbf{a}_1 = (1,1,0)^\top$, $\mathbf{a}_2 = (1,0,1)^\top$, $\mathbf{a}_3 = (0,1,1)^\top$.
Solution
Step 1: Normalize the first vector. $$\mathbf{q}_1 = \frac{\mathbf{a}_1}{\|\mathbf{a}_1\|} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,1,0)^\top.$$ Step 2: Subtract projection. $$\mathbf{v}_2 = \mathbf{a}_2 - \langle \mathbf{a}_2, \mathbf{q}_1 \rangle \mathbf{q}_1 = (1,0,1)^\top - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,1,0)^\top = (1,0,1)^\top - \frac{1}{2}(1,1,0)^\top = (\tfrac{1}{2}, -\tfrac{1}{2}, 1)^\top.$$ Normalize: $\|\mathbf{v}_2\| = \sqrt{\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} + 1} = \sqrt{\frac{3}{2}}$, so $\mathbf{q}_2 = \sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}(\tfrac{1}{2}, -\tfrac{1}{2}, 1)^\top = \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}(1,-1,2)^\top$.

Step 3: Third vector. $$\mathbf{v}_3 = \mathbf{a}_3 - \langle \mathbf{a}_3, \mathbf{q}_1 \rangle \mathbf{q}_1 - \langle \mathbf{a}_3, \mathbf{q}_2 \rangle \mathbf{q}_2.$$ $\langle \mathbf{a}_3, \mathbf{q}_1 \rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$, $\langle \mathbf{a}_3, \mathbf{q}_2 \rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}(-1+2) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}$. $$\mathbf{v}_3 = (0,1,1)^\top - \tfrac{1}{2}(1,1,0)^\top - \tfrac{1}{6}(1,-1,2)^\top = (-\tfrac{2}{3}, \tfrac{2}{3}, \tfrac{2}{3})^\top.$$ Normalize: $\mathbf{q}_3 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}(-1,1,1)^\top$.

Result: Orthonormal basis $\{\mathbf{q}_1, \mathbf{q}_2, \mathbf{q}_3\}$.
Gram-Schmidt 核心思想：对每个新向量，减去它在所有已处理向量上的投影，然后归一化。
公式：$\mathbf{v}_k = \mathbf{a}_k - \sum_{i=1}^{k-1} \langle \mathbf{a}_k, \mathbf{q}_i \rangle \mathbf{q}_i$，$\mathbf{q}_k = \mathbf{v}_k / \|\mathbf{v}_k\|$。
考试注意：计算容易出错，建议每步检查正交性（$\langle \mathbf{q}_i, \mathbf{q}_j \rangle = 0$ 对于 $i \neq j$）。
Q16
Define an orthogonal matrix and list three key properties.
Solution
$Q \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ is orthogonal if $Q^\top Q = I$ (equivalently $Q^{-1} = Q^\top$, or columns form an orthonormal basis).

Properties:
  1. Preserves length: $\|Q\mathbf{x}\| = \|\mathbf{x}\|$ for all $\mathbf{x}$.
  2. Preserves angles: $\langle Q\mathbf{x}, Q\mathbf{y} \rangle = \langle \mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y} \rangle$.
  3. Product of orthogonal matrices is orthogonal.
正交矩阵的列向量构成标准正交基。最重要的性质是保长（$\|Q\mathbf{x}\| = \|\mathbf{x}\|$）——即正交变换等价于旋转或反射。

7. Determinants & Linear Transformations

Q17
Compute $\det\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$.
Solution
Block diagonal matrix: $\det = \det\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&3\end{pmatrix} \cdot \det\begin{pmatrix}4&2\\1&5\end{pmatrix} = (6-1) \cdot (20-2) = 5 \cdot 18 = 90$.
分块对角矩阵的行列式等于各对角块行列式的乘积。这是行列式性质P2的重要应用。
Q18
If $A$ is $4 \times 4$ with $\det(A) = 3$, find (a) $\det(2A)$, (b) $\det(A^{-1})$, (c) $\det(A^2)$.
Solution
(a) $\det(2A) = 2^4 \det(A) = 16 \times 3 = 48$. (Scale all $n=4$ rows by 2, each multiplies determinant by 2.)
(b) $\det(A^{-1}) = 1/\det(A) = 1/3$.
(c) $\det(A^2) = (\det(A))^2 = 3^2 = 9$.
行列式关键性质：$\det(cA) = c^n \det(A)$（不是 $c\det(A)$！这是高频错误点）；$\det(A^{-1}) = 1/\det(A)$；$\det(AB) = \det(A)\det(B)$。
Q19
Give the definition of a linear transformation $T: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$.
Solution
$T$ is a linear transformation if for all $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$:
$$T(c\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}) = cT(\mathbf{u}) + T(\mathbf{v}).$$
Equivalently, (1) $T(\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}) = T(\mathbf{u}) + T(\mathbf{v})$ (additivity), and (2) $T(c\mathbf{u}) = cT(\mathbf{u})$ (homogeneity).

Every linear transformation can be represented by a matrix: $T(\mathbf{x}) = A\mathbf{x}$ where $A = [T(\mathbf{e}_1) \; T(\mathbf{e}_2) \; \cdots \; T(\mathbf{e}_n)]$.
线性变换 = 矩阵。任何线性变换 $T: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$ 都可以写成 $T(\mathbf{x}) = A\mathbf{x}$，其中 $A$ 的第 $j$ 列是 $T(\mathbf{e}_j)$。反过来，每个矩阵乘一个向量就是一个线性变换。

8. Four Fundamental Subspaces

Q20
For $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ (in RREF), find bases and dimensions of $C(A)$, $N(A)$, $\text{Row}(A)$, and $N(A^\top)$.
Solution
$A$ is $3 \times 4$, pivots at columns 1 and 3. Rank $r = 2$.

Column space $C(A)$: Pivot columns of original $A$ (columns 1 and 3). Basis: $\{(1,0,0)^\top, (0,1,0)^\top\}$. $\dim = 2$.
Nullspace $N(A)$: Free variables: $x_2, x_4$. From RREF: $x_1 + 2x_2 + x_4 = 0$, $x_3 + 3x_4 = 0$. $$\mathbf{x} = s\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix} + t\begin{pmatrix}-1\\0\\-3\\1\end{pmatrix}.$$ Basis: $\{(-2,1,0,0)^\top, (-1,0,-3,1)^\top\}$. $\dim = n - r = 4 - 2 = 2$.
Row space $\text{Row}(A)$: Non-zero rows of RREF. Basis: $\{(1,2,0,1), (0,0,1,3)\}$. $\dim = r = 2$.
Left nullspace $N(A^\top)$: $A^\top\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{0}$. Row 3 of $A$ is all zeros → $y_3$ free. Basis: $\{(0,0,1)^\top\}$. $\dim = m - r = 3 - 2 = 1$.
四个基本子空间的维数关系：对于 $m \times n$ 矩阵 $A$（秩为 $r$）：
$\dim(C(A)) = r$（列空间 ⊂ $\mathbb{R}^m$）
$\dim(N(A)) = n-r$（零空间 ⊂ $\mathbb{R}^n$）
$\dim(\text{Row}(A)) = r$（行空间 ⊂ $\mathbb{R}^n$）
$\dim(N(A^\top)) = m-r$（左零空间 ⊂ $\mathbb{R}^m$）

注意：列空间的基取原矩阵的pivot列（不是RREF的！），而行空间的基取RREF的非零行。
Q21
Prove that $\text{Row}(A) \perp N(A)$.
Proof
Let $\mathbf{r}$ be any row of $A$ and $\mathbf{x} \in N(A)$. By definition, $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$. The component corresponding to row $\mathbf{r}$ is $\langle \mathbf{r}, \mathbf{x} \rangle = 0$.
Since this holds for every row, $\mathbf{x} \perp \mathbf{r}$ for all rows $\mathbf{r}$.
Any vector in $\text{Row}(A)$ is a linear combination of rows: $\mathbf{w} = \sum c_i \mathbf{r}_i$.
Then $\langle \mathbf{w}, \mathbf{x} \rangle = \sum c_i \langle \mathbf{r}_i, \mathbf{x} \rangle = 0$. Thus $\mathbf{w} \perp \mathbf{x}$ for all $\mathbf{w} \in \text{Row}(A)$ and $\mathbf{x} \in N(A)$. ∎
零空间的几何含义：$A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$ 意味着 $\mathbf{x}$ 与 $A$ 的每一行正交，因此与行空间中的所有向量正交。所以零空间 = 行空间的正交补。这就是为什么 $\dim(N(A)) + \dim(\text{Row}(A)) = n$。

9. True / False & Conceptual Questions

Q22
True or False: If $A$ is invertible, then $\det(A^{-1}) = \det(A)$.
Answer: FALSE
The correct relation is $\det(A^{-1}) = 1/\det(A)$. They are equal only when $\det(A) = \pm 1$.
Example: $A = \begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, $\det(A)=2$, $\det(A^{-1}) = 1/2 \neq 2$.
Q23
True or False: Every square matrix has a full set of real eigenvalues.
Answer: FALSE
Counterexample: $A = \begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$ has eigenvalues $\lambda = i, -i$ (complex, not real).
Only symmetric matrices are guaranteed to have all real eigenvalues (Spectral Theorem).
Q24
True or False: If $A$ and $B$ are $n \times n$ with $AB = I$, then $BA = I$.
Answer: TRUE
For square matrices, one-sided inverse implies two-sided inverse. $AB = I$ → $B = A^{-1}$ → $BA = I$.
(This is not true for rectangular matrices.)
Q25
True or False: If $A$ has $n$ distinct eigenvalues, then $A$ is diagonalizable.
Answer: TRUE
Distinct eigenvalues → eigenvectors are linearly independent → $n$ linearly independent eigenvectors → eigenbasis exists → diagonalizable.
This is a sufficient condition, not necessary.
Q26
True or False: Eigenvectors corresponding to distinct eigenvalues are always orthogonal.
Answer: FALSE
They are linearly independent, but not necessarily orthogonal.
Orthogonality holds for symmetric (or normal) matrices specifically.
Counterexample: $A = \begin{pmatrix}3&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ has eigenvectors $(1,0)^\top$ (for $\lambda=3$) and $(-1,1)^\top$ (for $\lambda=2$), which are not orthogonal (dot product = -1).
Q27
True or False: If $A$ is positive definite, then $A$ is invertible.
Answer: TRUE
PD → all eigenvalues $\lambda_i > 0$ → $\det(A) = \prod \lambda_i > 0$ → $\det(A) \neq 0$ → invertible.
Also: if $A$ were singular, $\exists \mathbf{x} \neq \mathbf{0}$ with $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$ → $\mathbf{x}^\top A\mathbf{x} = 0$, contradicting PD.
Q28
True or False: Two similar matrices have the same eigenvalues.
Answer: TRUE
If $B = P^{-1}AP$, then $\det(\lambda I - B) = \det(\lambda I - P^{-1}AP) = \det(P^{-1}(\lambda I - A)P) = \det(\lambda I - A)$.
Same characteristic polynomial → same eigenvalues with same multiplicities.
Q29
True or False: $\det(cA) = c\det(A)$ for any $n \times n$ matrix $A$.
Answer: FALSE
The correct formula is $\det(cA) = c^n \det(A)$, since scalar multiplication multiplies all $n$ rows by $c$, each contributing a factor of $c$.
Example: $A = I_2$, $\det(2I_2) = 4$, not $2$.
Q30
True or False: The nullspace $N(A)$ is always a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^m$ (where $A$ is $m \times n$).
Answer: FALSE
$N(A) = \{\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n : A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}\}$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$ (the domain), not $\mathbb{R}^m$.
$C(A)$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^m$ (the codomain). $N(A^\top)$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^m$.

10. Mixed / Comprehensive Problems

Q31
Rabbit-Wolf Population Model: $x_{k+1} = 1.2x_k - 0.2y_k$, $y_{k+1} = 0.3x_k + 0.5y_k$. With $(x_0, y_0) = (100, 40)$, find $(x_k, y_k)$.
Solution
Step 1: Matrix form. $\mathbf{z}_{k+1} = A\mathbf{z}_k$ where $A = \begin{pmatrix}1.2&-0.2\\0.3&0.5\end{pmatrix}$.
Step 2: Eigenvalues. $\det(\lambda I - A) = (\lambda-1.2)(\lambda-0.5) + 0.06 = \lambda^2 - 1.7\lambda + 0.66 = (\lambda-1.1)(\lambda-0.6)$.
$\lambda_1 = 1.1$ (dominant), $\lambda_2 = 0.6$.
Step 3: Eigenvectors. For $\lambda = 1.1$: $\mathbf{v}_1 = (2, 1)^\top$. For $\lambda = 0.6$: $\mathbf{v}_2 = (1, 3)^\top$.
Step 4: Express initial state in eigenbasis. Solve $\alpha(2,1)^\top + \beta(1,3)^\top = (100,40)^\top$: $2\alpha + \beta = 100$, $\alpha + 3\beta = 40$. Solution: $\alpha = 52$, $\beta = -4$.
Step 5: Evolution. $\mathbf{z}_k = A^k\mathbf{z}_0 = 52(1.1)^k(2,1)^\top - 4(0.6)^k(1,3)^\top$.
$$x_k = 104(1.1)^k - 4(0.6)^k, \quad y_k = 52(1.1)^k - 12(0.6)^k.$$
As $k \to \infty$, $(0.6)^k \to 0$, so the population grows proportionally to $(1.1)^k$ along the direction $(2,1)$.
动力系统（Diagonalization应用）：这是对角化最经典的应用。步骤：①写成矩阵形式 $\mathbf{z}_{k+1} = A\mathbf{z}_k$ ②求特征值、特征向量 ③将初值用特征向量线性表示 ④ $\mathbf{z}_k = \alpha\lambda_1^k\mathbf{v}_1 + \beta\lambda_2^k\mathbf{v}_2$。
长期行为：由最大特征值（spectral radius）主导——当 $k$ 很大时，系统趋近于沿主特征向量方向以 $\lambda_{\max}$ 速度增长或衰减。
Q32
Compute the Frobenius norm of $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ and verify $\|B\|_F = \sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(B^\top B)}$.
Solution
$\|B\|_F = \sqrt{1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{1 + 4 + 9 + 16} = \sqrt{30}$.
Verify: $B^\top B = \begin{pmatrix}1&3\\2&4\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}10&14\\14&20\end{pmatrix}$.
$\operatorname{tr}(B^\top B) = 10 + 20 = 30$. So $\|B\|_F = \sqrt{30}$ ✓.
Q33
Express $\|uu^\top\|_F$ in terms of $\|u\|$ for $u \in \mathbb{R}^n$.
Solution
$(uu^\top)_{ij} = u_i u_j$. Then: $$\|uu^\top\|_F^2 = \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^n (u_i u_j)^2 = \left(\sum_{i=1}^n u_i^2\right)\left(\sum_{j=1}^n u_j^2\right) = \|u\|^2 \cdot \|u\|^2 = \|u\|^4.$$ Thus $\|uu^\top\|_F = \|u\|^2$.
秩-1矩阵 $uu^\top$（外积）的Frobenius范数 = $\|u\|^2$。这个结论经常在SVD上下文中使用。
Q34
Let $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ be symmetric. Prove that eigenvectors corresponding to distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal.
Proof
Let $A\mathbf{v}_1 = \lambda_1\mathbf{v}_1$, $A\mathbf{v}_2 = \lambda_2\mathbf{v}_2$, with $\lambda_1 \neq \lambda_2$.
Since $A$ is symmetric: $A^\top = A$.
Consider $\lambda_1 \langle\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2\rangle = \langle\lambda_1\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2\rangle = \langle A\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2\rangle = \mathbf{v}_1^\top A^\top \mathbf{v}_2 = \mathbf{v}_1^\top A \mathbf{v}_2 = \langle\mathbf{v}_1, \lambda_2\mathbf{v}_2\rangle = \lambda_2\langle\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2\rangle$.
Thus $(\lambda_1 - \lambda_2)\langle\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2\rangle = 0$. Since $\lambda_1 \neq \lambda_2$, we must have $\langle\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2\rangle = 0$. ∎
核心技巧：利用对称性 $A^\top = A$ 将内积中的$A$从一边"搬到"另一边，然后利用特征值不同推出内积为零。这是谱定理的基础证明之一，考试高频。
Q35
If $A = R^\top R$ where $R \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ is invertible, prove $A$ is positive definite.
Proof
For any $\mathbf{x} \neq \mathbf{0}$: $\mathbf{x}^\top A \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{x}^\top (R^\top R)\mathbf{x} = (R\mathbf{x})^\top (R\mathbf{x}) = \|R\mathbf{x}\|^2$.
Since $R$ is invertible and $\mathbf{x} \neq \mathbf{0}$, $R\mathbf{x} \neq \mathbf{0}$. Thus $\|R\mathbf{x}\|^2 > 0$.
Therefore $\mathbf{x}^\top A\mathbf{x} > 0$ for all $\mathbf{x} \neq \mathbf{0}$, so $A$ is positive definite. ∎
$A = R^\top R$（$R$可逆）是正定的等价条件。这一分解就是Cholesky分解。注意：必须是可逆的$R$，若$R$不可逆则为半正定。

Common Pitfalls

• det(cA) = cn det(A), NOT c·det(A)
• (AB)T = BTAT, NOT ATBT
• (AB)-1 = B-1A-1, NOT A-1B-1
• Column space basis = pivot columns of ORIGINAL A, not RREF
• Eigenvectors ≠ orthogonal (unless symmetric)
• N(A) ⊂ ℝn, C(A) ⊂ ℝm (don't mix up!)

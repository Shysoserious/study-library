📘 Week 12 — Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors & the Spectral Theorem
Q1
Give the definitions of eigenvalues and eigenvectors.
Let \(A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) be a square matrix. A nonzero vector \(v \in \mathbb{C}^n\) is called an eigenvector of \(A\) if there exists a scalar \(\lambda \in \mathbb{C}\) such that \(A v = \lambda v\). The scalar \(\lambda\) is the corresponding eigenvalue. Intuitively, eigenvectors are nonzero vectors that are only scaled (stretched/compressed) by \(A\), not rotated.
设 \(A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) 是方阵。非零向量 \(v\) 称为 \(A\) 的特征向量，若存在标量 \(\lambda\) 使得 \(A v = \lambda v\)。\(\lambda\) 称为特征值。直观上，特征向量是被 \(A\) 乘之后只伸缩不旋转的非零向量。
Q2
Give the definition of the characteristic polynomial of a matrix A.
The characteristic polynomial of \(A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) is \(p_A(\lambda) = \det(\lambda I - A)\) (or \(\det(A - \lambda I)\)). It is a degree-\(n\) polynomial in \(\lambda\). Using \(\det(\lambda I - A)\) gives a monic polynomial (leading coefficient 1).
矩阵 \(A\) 的特征多项式定义为 \(p_A(\lambda) = \det(\lambda I - A)\)（或 \(\det(A - \lambda I)\)），是关于 \(\lambda\) 的 \(n\) 次多项式。\(\det(\lambda I - A)\) 给出首一多项式（最高次项系数为 1）。
Q3
Prove that the eigenvalues of a matrix are the roots of its characteristic polynomial.
By definition, \(\lambda\) is an eigenvalue of \(A\) iff \(\exists v \neq 0\) s.t. \(A v = \lambda v \iff (\lambda I - A)v = 0\). A nonzero solution exists iff \(\lambda I - A\) is singular, i.e., \(\det(\lambda I - A) = 0\). Hence \(\lambda\) satisfies the characteristic equation, meaning it is a root of \(p_A(\lambda)\).
根据定义，\(\lambda\) 是特征值当且仅当存在非零 \(v\) 使 \((\lambda I - A)v = 0\)，这等价于 \(\lambda I - A\) 奇异，即 \(\det(\lambda I - A) = 0\)。所以 \(\lambda\) 满足特征方程，即特征多项式的根。
Q4
Give the steps to compute eigenvalues and eigenspaces of \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\).
  1. Compute \(p_A(\lambda) = \det(\lambda I - A)\).
  2. Find all roots \(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n\) — the eigenvalues (with multiplicities).
  3. For each distinct \(\lambda_i\), solve \((\lambda_i I - A) v = 0\) to get eigenspace \(E_{\lambda_i} = \ker(\lambda_i I - A)\).
  4. \(\dim(E_{\lambda_i})\) is the geometric multiplicity (\(\le\) algebraic multiplicity).
第一步：计算特征多项式。第二步：求所有根得到特征值。第三步：解齐次方程组得到特征空间。第四步：特征空间的维数是几何重数（不超过代数重数）。
Q5
Eigenvalues and eigenspaces of \(A = \begin{pmatrix}3&2\\-2&-2\end{pmatrix}\).
\(\det(\lambda I - A) = (\lambda - 3)(\lambda + 2) + 4 = \lambda^2 - \lambda - 2 = (\lambda - 2)(\lambda + 1)\).
Eigenvalues: \(\lambda_1 = 2,\; \lambda_2 = -1\).
For \(\lambda = 2\): \((2I - A)v = 0 \Rightarrow v = t(-2, 1)^\top\). \(E_2 = \operatorname{Span}\{(-2, 1)^\top\}\).
For \(\lambda = -1\): \((-I - A)v = 0 \Rightarrow v = s(1, -2)^\top\). \(E_{-1} = \operatorname{Span}\{(1, -2)^\top\}\).
特征多项式为 \(\lambda^2 - \lambda - 2 = (\lambda - 2)(\lambda + 1)\)。特征值为 2 和 -1。对应特征空间分别为 \(\operatorname{Span}\{(-2, 1)^\top\}\) 和 \(\operatorname{Span}\{(1, -2)^\top\}\)。
Q6
Eigenvalues of \(A = \begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}\).
\(\det(\lambda I - A) = \det\begin{pmatrix}\lambda&-1\\1&\lambda\end{pmatrix} = \lambda^2 + 1\). Eigenvalues are \(\lambda = i\) and \(\lambda = -i\) — purely imaginary, showing that a real matrix can have complex eigenvalues.
特征多项式为 \(\lambda^2 + 1\)，特征值为 \(i\) 和 \(-i\)（纯虚数）。这说明实矩阵可以有复特征值。
Q7
Eigenvalues and eigenspaces of \(A = \begin{pmatrix}2&1&-3\\0&3&4\\0&0&-1\end{pmatrix}\).
Since \(A\) is upper triangular, eigenvalues are the diagonal entries: \(\lambda = 2, 3, -1\).
\(E_2 = \operatorname{Span}\{(1,0,0)^\top\},\; E_3 = \operatorname{Span}\{(1,1,0)^\top\},\; E_{-1} = \operatorname{Span}\{(4,-3,3)^\top\}\).
上三角矩阵的特征值即对角线元素。三个特征空间分别由 \((1,0,0)^\top\), \((1,1,0)^\top\), \((4,-3,3)^\top\) 张成。
Q8
Eigenvalues of \(A = \begin{pmatrix}2&1&1\\1&2&1\\1&1&2\end{pmatrix}\).
\(A = J + I\) where \(J\) is the all-ones matrix. \(J\) has eigenvalues \(3, 0, 0\), so \(A\) has eigenvalues \(4, 1, 1\). Vector \((1,1,1)^\top\) is eigenvector for \(\lambda = 4\); vectors orthogonal to it (e.g., \((1,-1,0)^\top\)) are eigenvectors for \(\lambda = 1\).
\(A = J + I\)，\(J\) 是全 1 矩阵。\(J\) 的特征值为 3, 0, 0，所以 \(A\) 的特征值为 4, 1, 1。
Q9
Fundamental Theorem of Algebra and consequences for eigenvalues.
FTA: Every non-constant polynomial with complex coefficients has at least one complex root. Equivalently, every degree-\(n\) polynomial factors as \(p(z) = c(z - r_1)\cdots(z - r_n)\), \(r_i \in \mathbb{C}\).

Consequence: The characteristic polynomial of an \(n \times n\) matrix is degree \(n\), so it always has \(n\) complex roots (counting multiplicities). Hence every square matrix has exactly \(n\) eigenvalues in \(\mathbb{C}\) (with algebraic multiplicities).
代数基本定理：非常数的复系数多项式至少有一个复根。因此 \(n\) 次特征多项式在复数域中恰有 \(n\) 个根。每个方阵在 \(\mathbb{C}\) 中恰好有 \(n\) 个特征值（按代数重数计）。
Q10
Difference between a set and a multiset. Why important for eigenvalues?
A set has no repeated elements; a multiset allows repetitions and tracks multiplicities. For eigenvalues, repetitions matter — a matrix may have repeated eigenvalues. The spectrum is best described as a multiset \(\{\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n\}\) where each eigenvalue appears as many times as its algebraic multiplicity.
集合不允许重复，多重集允许重复并记录重数。特征值可能有重复，所以谱最好用多重集描述，每个特征值按代数重数出现。
Q11
Complex numbers: (i) characterization of \(i\); (ii) complex conjugate; (iii) modulus.
(i) \(i\) satisfies \(i^2 = -1\); it is a root of \(x^2 + 1 = 0\).
(ii) For \(z = a + bi\), the complex conjugate is \(\bar{z} = a - bi\).
(iii) \(|z| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = \sqrt{z\bar{z}}\).
(i) \(i\) 满足 \(i^2 = -1\)。(ii) 共轭复数 \(\bar{z} = a - bi\)。(iii) 模 \(|z| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = \sqrt{z\bar{z}}\)。
Q12
Is the standard basis of \(\mathbb{R}^3\) also a basis of \(\mathbb{C}^3\)?
Yes. \(e_1, e_2, e_3 \in \mathbb{C}^3\) (since \(\mathbb{R} \subset \mathbb{C}\)). They span \(\mathbb{C}^3\) (any complex vector is a complex linear combination) and are linearly independent over \(\mathbb{C}\). So \(\{e_1, e_2, e_3\}\) is a basis of \(\mathbb{C}^3\).
是的。\(\mathbb{R} \subset \mathbb{C}\)，所以 \(e_1, e_2, e_3\) 也属于 \(\mathbb{C}^3\)，它们张成 \(\mathbb{C}^3\) 且在复数域上线性无关。
Q13
Are \(u = (1, i)^\top\) and \(v = (i, -1)^\top\) linearly independent?
Check \(\alpha u + \beta v = 0\): \(\alpha(1,i)^\top + \beta(i,-1)^\top = (\alpha + i\beta,\; i\alpha - \beta)^\top = (0,0)^\top\).
From the first entry: \(\alpha = -i\beta\). Substituting into the second: \(i(-i\beta) - \beta = \beta - \beta = 0\). So \(\beta = 1, \alpha = -i\) works. Hence they are linearly dependent. Indeed, \(v = i u\).
存在非零系数 \(\alpha = -i, \beta = 1\) 使线性组合为零，所以 \(u\) 和 \(v\) 线性相关。实际上 \(v = i u\)。
Q14
Give the definition of an eigenbasis.
An eigenbasis of \(A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) is a basis of \(\mathbb{C}^n\) consisting entirely of eigenvectors of \(A\). Equivalently, a set of \(n\) linearly independent eigenvectors spanning \(\mathbb{C}^n\).
特征基是由 \(A\) 的特征向量组成的 \(\mathbb{C}^n\) 的一组基，即 \(n\) 个线性无关的特征向量张成 \(\mathbb{C}^n\)。
Q15
Eigenvalues/eigenspaces of \(A = \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\). Does it admit an eigenbasis?
\(\lambda = 1\) (algebraic multiplicity 2). Solve \((I - A)v = 0\): \(\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}v = 0 \Rightarrow v_2 = 0\). So \(E_1 = \operatorname{Span}\{(1,0)^\top\}\), dimension 1.

Geometric multiplicity (1) < algebraic multiplicity (2), so we cannot find 2 linearly independent eigenvectors. \(A\) does not admit an eigenbasis and is not diagonalizable (a Jordan block).
特征值 \(\lambda = 1\)（代数重数 2），但几何重数只有 1，所以不存在 2 个线性无关的特征向量，没有特征基，不可对角化（这是若尔当块）。
Q16
Eigenvalues/eigenspaces of \(A = \begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\). Does it admit an eigenbasis?
Eigenvalues: \(\lambda = 1\) (eigenvector \((1,1)^\top\)) and \(\lambda = -1\) (eigenvector \((1,-1)^\top\)). These two are linearly independent and form an eigenbasis. Yes, \(A\) admits an eigenbasis (is diagonalizable).
特征值为 1 和 -1，特征向量 \((1,1)^\top\) 和 \((1,-1)^\top\) 线性无关，构成特征基。\(A\) 可对角化。
Q17
Given diagonal \(D\) and \(X \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\), express \(DX\) and \(XD\).
Let \(D = \operatorname{Diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)\).
\(DX\) multiplies the \(i\)-th row of \(X\) by \(\lambda_i\): \((DX)_{ij} = \lambda_i X_{ij}\).
\(XD\) multiplies the \(j\)-th column of \(X\) by \(\lambda_j\): \((XD)_{ij} = X_{ij} \lambda_j\).
\(DX\) 将 \(X\) 的第 \(i\) 行乘以 \(\lambda_i\)；\(XD\) 将 \(X\) 的第 \(j\) 列乘以 \(\lambda_j\)。
Q18
Express \(A v_i = \lambda_i v_i\) for \(i=1,\dots,n\) in matrix form.
Let \(V = [v_1 \; \cdots \; v_n] \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) and \(\Lambda = \operatorname{Diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)\). Then the \(n\) equations become \[ A V = V \Lambda. \] Indeed, the \(j\)-th column of \(A V\) is \(A v_j = \lambda_j v_j\), which matches the \(j\)-th column of \(V \Lambda\).
令 \(V = [v_1 \; \cdots \; v_n]\)，\(\Lambda = \operatorname{Diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)\)，则 \(A V = V \Lambda\)。
Q19
Show that if \(A\) admits an orthonormal eigenbasis, then \(A\) is symmetric.
Let \(\{q_1, \dots, q_n\}\) be an orthonormal eigenbasis, \(Q = [q_1 \; \cdots \; q_n]\) (orthogonal: \(Q^\top Q = I\)). From \(A Q = Q \Lambda\) we get \(A = Q \Lambda Q^\top\). Then \[ A^\top = (Q \Lambda Q^\top)^\top = Q \Lambda^\top Q^\top = Q \Lambda Q^\top = A, \] since \(\Lambda\) is diagonal (\(\Lambda^\top = \Lambda\)). Thus \(A\) is symmetric.
设 \(Q\) 是以标准正交特征向量为列的正交矩阵。由 \(A = Q \Lambda Q^\top\) 取转置得 \(A^\top = A\)，即 \(A\) 对称。
Q20
Precise statement of the Spectral Theorem for symmetric matrices.
Spectral Theorem: If \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\) is symmetric (\(A^\top = A\)), then:
1. All eigenvalues of \(A\) are real.
2. \(A\) is orthogonally diagonalizable: \(\exists\) orthogonal \(Q\) and diagonal \(\Lambda\) s.t. \(A = Q \Lambda Q^\top\). Equivalently, \(\mathbb{R}^n\) has an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors of \(A\).
实对称矩阵的谱定理：(1) 所有特征值为实数。(2) \(A\) 可正交对角化：存在正交矩阵 \(Q\) 和对角矩阵 \(\Lambda\) 使得 \(A = Q \Lambda Q^\top\)。
Q21
Show eigenvectors for distinct eigenvalues of a symmetric matrix are orthogonal.
Let \(A v_1 = \lambda_1 v_1\), \(A v_2 = \lambda_2 v_2\) with \(\lambda_1 \neq \lambda_2\). Since \(A^\top = A\), \[ \lambda_1(v_1^\top v_2) = (A v_1)^\top v_2 = v_1^\top A v_2 = \lambda_2(v_1^\top v_2). \] Thus \((\lambda_1 - \lambda_2)(v_1^\top v_2) = 0\). Since \(\lambda_1 \neq \lambda_2\), we get \(v_1^\top v_2 = 0\) — orthogonal.
利用对称性：\(\lambda_1(v_1^\top v_2) = (A v_1)^\top v_2 = v_1^\top A v_2 = \lambda_2(v_1^\top v_2)\)，所以 \((\lambda_1 - \lambda_2)(v_1^\top v_2) = 0\)，因 \(\lambda_1 \neq \lambda_2\) 故 \(v_1^\top v_2 = 0\)。
📗 Week 13 — Similar Matrices, Diagonalization, SVD & Frobenius Norm
Q1
Give the definition of similar matrices.
\(A, B \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) are similar if \(\exists\) invertible \(P \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) such that \(B = P^{-1} A P\). Similarity is an equivalence relation — similar matrices represent the same linear transformation in different bases.
若存在可逆矩阵 \(P\) 使 \(B = P^{-1} A P\)，则 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 相似。相似矩阵表示同一个线性变换在不同基下的矩阵。
Q2
Show if \(\lambda\) is an eigenvalue of \(A\), it is also an eigenvalue of similar \(B\).
\(B = P^{-1} A P\). If \(A v = \lambda v\), then \[ B (P^{-1} v) = P^{-1} A P (P^{-1} v) = P^{-1} A v = P^{-1} (\lambda v) = \lambda (P^{-1} v). \] So \(\lambda\) is an eigenvalue of \(B\) with eigenvector \(P^{-1} v\).
若 \(B = P^{-1} A P\) 且 \(A v = \lambda v\)，则 \(B (P^{-1} v) = \lambda (P^{-1} v)\)，所以 \(\lambda\) 也是 \(B\) 的特征值。
Q3
Show similar matrices have the same multiset of eigenvalues.
\(\det(\lambda I - B) = \det(\lambda I - P^{-1} A P) = \det(P^{-1}(\lambda I - A)P) = \det(P^{-1})\det(\lambda I - A)\det(P) = \det(\lambda I - A)\). Having the same characteristic polynomial implies the same eigenvalues with the same multiplicities.
相似矩阵有相同的特征多项式，从而有相同的特征值多重集（相同的特征值及相同的代数重数）。
Q4
Give the definition of a diagonalizable matrix.
\(A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) is diagonalizable if it is similar to a diagonal matrix: \(\exists\) invertible \(P\) and diagonal \(D\) such that \(A = P D P^{-1}\).
\(A\) 可对角化如果它相似于一个对角矩阵，即存在可逆矩阵 \(P\) 和对角矩阵 \(D\) 使 \(A = P D P^{-1}\)。
Q5
N&S condition for diagonalizability involving eigenvectors.
\(A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\) is diagonalizable iff it admits an eigenbasis — a basis of \(\mathbb{R}^n\) consisting of eigenvectors of \(A\). Equivalently, for every eigenvalue, geometric multiplicity = algebraic multiplicity.
\(A\) 可对角化当且仅当它有一个特征基（由特征向量构成的基），等价地说每个特征值的几何重数等于代数重数。
Q6
Prove \(A\) is diagonalizable iff it admits an eigenbasis.
\((\Rightarrow)\) If \(A = P D P^{-1}\) with \(D = \operatorname{Diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)\), the columns of \(P\) are eigenvectors and form an eigenbasis.
\((\Leftarrow)\) If \(\{v_1, \dots, v_n\}\) is an eigenbasis with \(A v_j = \lambda_j v_j\), let \(P = [v_1 \; \cdots \; v_n]\) and \(D = \operatorname{Diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)\). Then \(A P = P D\), so \(A = P D P^{-1}\).
\((\Rightarrow)\) 若 \(A = P D P^{-1}\)，则 \(P\) 的列构成特征基。\((\Leftarrow)\) 若有特征基，令 \(P\) 以特征向量为列，\(D\) 为对角矩阵，则 \(A P = P D\)，即 \(A = P D P^{-1}\)。
Q7
Are all square matrices diagonalizable? Counterexample.
No. Counterexample: \(A = \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\). Eigenvalue \(\lambda = 1\) has algebraic multiplicity 2 but geometric multiplicity 1 (eigenspace is 1-dimensional). So \(A\) is not diagonalizable (a defective matrix / Jordan block).
不是。反例：\(A = \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\)，特征值 1 的代数重数为 2 但几何重数为 1，不可对角化。
Q8
Give a sufficient condition for diagonalizability involving eigenvalues.
If \(A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) has \(n\) distinct eigenvalues, then \(A\) is diagonalizable. (Sufficient but not necessary — repeated eigenvalues can still yield diagonalizability if geometric multiplicities are full.)
若 \(A\) 有 \(n\) 个互不相同的特征值，则 \(A\) 可对角化。这是充分条件非必要条件。
Q9
Same multiset of eigenvalues ⇒ similar?
No. Counterexample: \(A = \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\) and \(B = I\) both have eigenvalues \(\{1, 1\}\) but are not similar (if they were, \(A = I\), contradiction). Similarity requires the same Jordan form — same algebraic and geometric multiplicities.
不一定。反例中两个矩阵都有特征值 \(\{1, 1\}\) 但不相似。相似需要相同的若尔当标准型，即代数重数和几何重数都要相同。
Q10
If \(A\) is similar to diagonal \(D\), give \(A^k\) formula.
If \(A = P D P^{-1}\) with \(D = \operatorname{Diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)\), then \[ A^k = P D^k P^{-1},\quad D^k = \operatorname{Diag}(\lambda_1^k, \dots, \lambda_n^k). \] This gives an efficient way to compute matrix powers via diagonalization.
若 \(A = P D P^{-1}\)，则 \(A^k = P D^k P^{-1}\)，其中 \(D^k = \operatorname{Diag}(\lambda_1^k, \dots, \lambda_n^k)\)。
Q11
Rabbits & wolves population: find \(x_k, y_k\).
System: \(x_{k+1} = 1.2 x_k - 0.2 y_k,\; y_{k+1} = 0.3 x_k + 0.5 y_k\). Given eigenvectors \(u = (2,1)^\top\) for \(\lambda_1 = 1.1\), \(v = (1,3)^\top\) for \(\lambda_2 = 0.6\). Initial: \((x_0, y_0) = (100, 40)\).

Express \((100, 40) = \alpha u + \beta v\): solving gives \(\alpha = 52, \beta = -4\). Then \[ \begin{pmatrix}x_k\\y_k\end{pmatrix} = A^k\begin{pmatrix}100\\40\end{pmatrix} = 52(1.1)^k\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix} - 4(0.6)^k\begin{pmatrix}1\\3\end{pmatrix}. \] Hence \(x_k = 104(1.1)^k - 4(0.6)^k\), \(y_k = 52(1.1)^k - 12(0.6)^k\).
将初始向量分解为特征向量组合 \((100,40) = 52u - 4v\)，则 \((x_k, y_k)^\top = 52\lambda_1^k u - 4\lambda_2^k v\)，代入得 \(x_k = 104(1.1)^k - 4(0.6)^k\)，\(y_k = 52(1.1)^k - 12(0.6)^k\)。
Q12
Formulas for sum and product of eigenvalues.
For \(A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}\) with eigenvalues \(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n\) (with multiplicities): \[ \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i = \operatorname{tr}(A) = \sum_{i=1}^n A_{ii},\qquad \prod_{i=1}^n \lambda_i = \det(A). \]
特征值之和 = \(A\) 的迹（对角线元素之和），特征值之积 = \(A\) 的行列式。
Q13
Express \(\operatorname{EIG}(A^k)\) and \(\operatorname{EIG}(A^{-1})\).
Let \(\operatorname{EIG}(A) = \{\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n\}\). Then:
  • \(\operatorname{EIG}(A^k) = \{\lambda_1^k, \dots, \lambda_n^k\}\) for any \(k \ge 1\).
  • \(\operatorname{EIG}(A^{-1}) = \{\lambda_1^{-1}, \dots, \lambda_n^{-1}\}\) if \(A\) is invertible.
Proof for \(A^k\) (diagonalizable case): \(A = P D P^{-1} \Rightarrow A^k = P D^k P^{-1}\), so eigenvalues are \(\lambda_i^k\).
\(\operatorname{EIG}(A^k) = \{\lambda_i^k\}\)，\(\operatorname{EIG}(A^{-1}) = \{\lambda_i^{-1}\}\)（若可逆）。证明利用对角化：\(A^k = P D^k P^{-1}\)。
Q14
(i) Are eigenvectors for distinct eigenvalues always orthogonal? (ii) Do eigenvalues make sense for rectangular matrices?
(i) No. They are always linearly independent, but orthogonal only for symmetric (normal) matrices.
(ii) No. The definition \(A v = \lambda v\) requires \(A\) to be square — otherwise \(A v\) and \(v\) live in different-dimensional spaces.
(i) 不同特征值的特征向量一定线性无关但不一定正交，正交性只对对称矩阵成立。(ii) 特征值的定义要求方阵，对长方形矩阵无意义。
Q15
State the compact SVD of \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times m}\) of rank \(r\).
The compact SVD is \[ A = U_r \Sigma_r V_r^\top = \sum_{i=1}^r \sigma_i u_i v_i^\top, \] where \(U_r \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times r}\), \(V_r \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times r}\) have orthonormal columns, and \(\Sigma_r = \operatorname{Diag}(\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_r)\) with \(\sigma_1 \ge \cdots \ge \sigma_r > 0\).
紧凑 SVD：\(A = U_r \Sigma_r V_r^\top = \sum_{i=1}^r \sigma_i u_i v_i^\top\)，其中 \(U_r, V_r\) 列标准正交，\(\Sigma_r\) 包含正奇异值。
Q16
Definition of the Frobenius norm \(\|B\|_F\).
For \(B \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}\), \[ \|B\|_F = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^m \sum_{j=1}^n B_{ij}^2}, \] i.e., the square root of the sum of squares of all entries — the Euclidean norm of the vectorized matrix.
Frobenius 范数是矩阵所有元素平方和的平方根，即将矩阵展平为向量的欧几里得范数。
Q17
Express \(\|B\|_F\) using trace; prove for \(m=n=2\).
\(\|B\|_F = \sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(B^\top B)}\). For \(B = \begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\): \(B^\top B = \begin{pmatrix}a^2+c^2&ab+cd\\ab+cd&b^2+d^2\end{pmatrix}\), so \(\operatorname{tr}(B^\top B) = a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2 = \|B\|_F^2\).
\(\|B\|_F = \sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(B^\top B)}\)。对 \(2 \times 2\) 矩阵直接展开验证即可。
Q18
Given \(u \in \mathbb{R}^n\), give \(\|u u^\top\|_F\).
\(\|u u^\top\|_F^2 = \sum_{i,j} (u_i u_j)^2 = (\sum_i u_i^2)(\sum_j u_j^2) = \|u\|^4\), so \(\|u u^\top\|_F = \|u\|^2\).
\(\|u u^\top\|_F^2 = \sum_{i,j} (u_i u_j)^2 = (\sum_i u_i^2)(\sum_j u_j^2) = \|u\|^4\)，所以 \(\|u u^\top\|_F = \|u\|^2\)。
Q19
Express \(\|B\|_F\) in terms of singular values \(\sigma_i\).
\(\|B\|_F = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^r \sigma_i^2}\).
Proof for \(r=2\): If \(B = \sigma_1 u_1 v_1^\top + \sigma_2 u_2 v_2^\top\) with orthonormal sets, then \[ B^\top B = \sigma_1^2 v_1 v_1^\top + \sigma_2^2 v_2 v_2^\top, \] and \(\operatorname{tr}(B^\top B) = \sigma_1^2 \|v_1\|^2 + \sigma_2^2 \|v_2\|^2 = \sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2\). Hence \(\|B\|_F = \sqrt{\sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2}\).
Frobenius 范数等于所有奇异值平方和的平方根：\(\|B\|_F = \sqrt{\sum \sigma_i^2}\)。
📙 Week 14 — SVD Details, Quadratic Forms & Positive Definiteness
Q1
Identify the four fundamental subspaces from full SVD.
Given \(A = U \Sigma V^\top\) with \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}\), rank \(r\):
  • \(\operatorname{Span}(v_1, \dots, v_r) = \operatorname{Row}(A)\) — row space (in \(\mathbb{R}^n\)).
  • \(\operatorname{Span}(v_{r+1}, \dots, v_n) = N(A)\) — nullspace (in \(\mathbb{R}^n\)).
  • \(\operatorname{Span}(u_1, \dots, u_r) = C(A)\) — column space (in \(\mathbb{R}^m\)).
  • \(\operatorname{Span}(u_{r+1}, \dots, u_m) = N(A^\top)\) — left nullspace (in \(\mathbb{R}^m\)).
\(V\) 的前 \(r\) 列张成行空间，后 \(n-r\) 列张成零空间；\(U\) 的前 \(r\) 列张成列空间，后 \(m-r\) 列张成左零空间。
Q2
Steps to compute the full SVD of \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}\).
  1. Compute \(A^\top A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\) (symmetric PSD).
  2. Find eigenvalues \(\lambda_1 \ge \cdots \ge \lambda_n \ge 0\) of \(A^\top A\). Singular values: \(\sigma_i = \sqrt{\lambda_i}\) for \(i \le r = \operatorname{rank}(A)\), and \(\sigma_i = 0\) for \(i > r\).
  3. Find orthonormal eigenvectors \(\{v_1, \dots, v_n\}\) of \(A^\top A\) — the right singular vectors.
  4. For \(i = 1, \dots, r\): \(u_i = \frac{1}{\sigma_i} A v_i\). Extend to orthonormal basis \(\{u_1, \dots, u_m\}\) of \(\mathbb{R}^m\).
  5. Assemble \(U = [u_1 \; \cdots \; u_m]\), \(V = [v_1 \; \cdots \; v_n]\), \(\Sigma\) as \(m \times n\) rectangular diagonal with \(\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_r\) on the diagonal.
1. 计算 \(A^\top A\)；2. 求其特征值得奇异值；3. 求右奇异向量；4. 构造左奇异向量；5. 组装 \(U, \Sigma, V\)。
Q3
Compute full SVD of \(A = \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\).
\(A^\top A = \begin{pmatrix}2&2\\2&2\end{pmatrix}\). Eigenvalues: \(4, 0\) → singular values: \(\sigma_1 = 2\), rank \(r = 1\).

\(v_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,1)^\top\), \(v_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,-1)^\top\).
\(u_1 = \frac{1}{\sigma_1} A v_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,1,0)^\top\). Extend: \(u_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,-1,0)^\top\), \(u_3 = (0,0,1)^\top\).

Full SVD: \[ A = \begin{pmatrix} 1/\sqrt{2} & 1/\sqrt{2} & 0\\ 1/\sqrt{2} & -1/\sqrt{2} & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix}2 & 0\\0 & 0\\0 & 0\end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1/\sqrt{2} & 1/\sqrt{2}\\ 1/\sqrt{2} & -1/\sqrt{2} \end{pmatrix}^\top. \]
计算 \(A^\top A\) 得特征值 4 和 0，奇异值 \(\sigma_1 = 2\)。右奇异向量 \(v_1 = (1,1)^\top/\sqrt{2}\)，左奇异向量 \(u_1 = (1,1,0)^\top/\sqrt{2}\)，扩充后组装得到完整 SVD。
Q4
Relation between eigenvalues of \(A^\top A\) and \(AA^\top\); singular values.
For \(i \le \min(m,n)\), \(\lambda_i = \mu_i\) (nonzero eigenvalues of \(A^\top A\) and \(AA^\top\) coincide). If \(r = \operatorname{rank}(A)\), then \(\lambda_{r+1} = \cdots = \lambda_n = 0\) and \(\mu_{r+1} = \cdots = \mu_m = 0\). If \(m \ge n\), singular values \(\sigma_i = \sqrt{\lambda_i}\) for \(i = 1, \dots, n\).
\(A^\top A\) 和 \(AA^\top\) 的非零特征值完全相同。奇异值 \(\sigma_i = \sqrt{\lambda_i}\)（取 \(A^\top A\) 的特征值）。
Q5
Are right/left singular vectors eigenvectors of \(AA^\top\) or \(A^\top A\)?
Right singular vectors \(v_i\) are eigenvectors of \(A^\top A\): \(A^\top A v_i = \sigma_i^2 v_i\).
Left singular vectors \(u_i\) are eigenvectors of \(A A^\top\): \(A A^\top u_i = \sigma_i^2 u_i\).
右奇异向量是 \(A^\top A\) 的特征向量，左奇异向量是 \(A A^\top\) 的特征向量。
Q6
Is the number of singular values of an \(m \times n\) matrix equal to \(\min(m, n)\)?
Yes. In the full SVD, \(\Sigma\) is \(m \times n\) with \(\min(m, n)\) diagonal entries (counting zeros). The number of nonzero singular values equals the rank \(r \le \min(m, n)\).
完整 SVD 中奇异值（含零）的个数为 \(\min(m, n)\)，非零奇异值的个数等于秩 \(r\)。
Q7
Definition of a quadratic form.
A quadratic form is a function \(Q: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}\) defined by \(Q(x) = x^\top A x = \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^n A_{ij} x_i x_j\), where \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\) is symmetric (canonically).
二次型是 \(Q(x) = x^\top A x = \sum_{i,j} A_{ij} x_i x_j\) 形式的函数，其中 \(A\) 是对称矩阵。
Q8
Definitions: PSD, PD, indefinite matrices.
For symmetric \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\):
  • PSD: \(x^\top A x \ge 0\) for all \(x\).
  • PD: \(x^\top A x > 0\) for all nonzero \(x\).
  • Indefinite: \(x^\top A x\) takes both positive and negative values.
  • NSD: \(x^\top A x \le 0\) for all \(x\).
  • ND: \(x^\top A x < 0\) for all nonzero \(x\).
对对称矩阵：半正定 (PSD) 为 \(x^\top A x \ge 0\)；正定 (PD) 为非零时 > 0；不定为可正可负；半负定 (NSD) 为 ≤ 0；负定 (ND) 为非零时 < 0。
Q9
For diagonal \(D\), express \(x^\top D x\) and give PD condition.
\(x^\top D x = \sum_{i=1}^n d_i x_i^2\). \(D\) is PD iff all \(d_i > 0\); PSD iff all \(d_i \ge 0\).
\(x^\top D x = \sum d_i x_i^2\)。\(D\) 正定当且仅当所有 \(d_i > 0\)，半正定当且仅当所有 \(d_i \ge 0\)。
Q10
Classify each matrix as PSD, PD, I, NSD, or ND.
  1. \(\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix}\) → PD (both eigenvalues > 0)
  2. \(\begin{pmatrix}-2&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\) → NSD (eigenvalues -2, 0)
  3. \(\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\) → PSD & NSD (zero matrix)
  4. \(\begin{pmatrix}-3&0\\0&-7\end{pmatrix}\) → ND (both eigenvalues < 0)
  5. \(\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\) → PSD (eigenvalues 2, 0)
  6. \(\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\0&-2\end{pmatrix}\) → I (indefinite, eigenvalues 3, -2)
  7. \(\begin{pmatrix}-2&0\\0&-5\end{pmatrix}\) → ND (both eigenvalues < 0)
1. 正定 (PD)；2. 半负定 (NSD)；3. 既半正定又半负定；4. 负定 (ND)；5. 半正定 (PSD)；6. 不定 (I)；7. 负定 (ND)。
Q11
Complete the theorem (equivalent conditions for PD).
For symmetric \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\), the following are equivalent:
  • \(A\) is positive definite.
  • All eigenvalues of \(A\) are positive.
  • \(A = R^\top R\) for some invertible matrix \(R \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\).
实对称矩阵正定等价于：(1) 所有特征值为正；(2) 可写为 \(A = R^\top R\)（\(R\) 可逆）。
Q12
Show if all eigenvalues are positive, then \(A\) is PD.
By the Spectral Theorem, \(A = Q \Lambda Q^\top\) with \(\Lambda = \operatorname{Diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)\), \(\lambda_i > 0\). For any nonzero \(x\), \[ x^\top A x = x^\top Q \Lambda Q^\top x = (Q^\top x)^\top \Lambda (Q^\top x) = \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i (Q^\top x)_i^2 > 0, \] since at least one component of \(Q^\top x\) is nonzero and all \(\lambda_i > 0\). Thus \(A\) is PD.
由谱定理 \(A = Q \Lambda Q^\top\)，对任意非零 \(x\)，\(x^\top A x = \sum \lambda_i (Q^\top x)_i^2 > 0\)，所以 \(A\) 正定。
Q13
Show if \(A\) is PD, then all eigenvalues are positive.
Let \(\lambda\) be an eigenvalue with eigenvector \(v \neq 0\). Then \(A v = \lambda v\). Multiply by \(v^\top\) on the left: \(v^\top A v = \lambda \|v\|^2\). Since \(A\) is PD, \(v^\top A v > 0\), hence \(\lambda > 0\).
设 \(A v = \lambda v\)，则 \(v^\top A v = \lambda \|v\|^2 > 0\)（由正定性），所以 \(\lambda > 0\)。
Q14
Show if \(A = R^\top R\) with \(R\) invertible, then \(A\) is PD.
For any nonzero \(x\), \(x^\top A x = x^\top R^\top R x = \|R x\|^2\). Since \(R\) is invertible, \(R x \neq 0\), so \(\|R x\|^2 > 0\). Thus \(A\) is PD.
\(x^\top A x = \|R x\|^2\)，因 \(R\) 可逆故 \(R x \neq 0\)，所以范数平方 > 0，\(A\) 正定。
Q15
Show if \(A\) is PD, then \(A = R^\top R\) for some invertible \(R\).
By the Spectral Theorem, \(A = Q \Lambda Q^\top\) with \(\lambda_i > 0\). Let \(\Lambda^{1/2} = \operatorname{Diag}(\sqrt{\lambda_1}, \dots, \sqrt{\lambda_n})\) and set \(R = \Lambda^{1/2} Q^\top\). Then \(R\) is invertible and \[ R^\top R = Q \Lambda^{1/2} \Lambda^{1/2} Q^\top = Q \Lambda Q^\top = A. \] This is the Cholesky decomposition.
由谱定理 \(A = Q \Lambda Q^\top\)，取 \(R = \Lambda^{1/2} Q^\top\)，则 \(R^\top R = A\)。这就是乔列斯基分解。
Q16
Are all PD matrices invertible?
Yes. All eigenvalues \(\lambda_i > 0\), so \(\det(A) = \prod \lambda_i > 0\), hence \(\det(A) \neq 0\) and \(A\) is invertible. (If \(A\) were singular, \(\exists x \neq 0\) with \(A x = 0\), giving \(x^\top A x = 0\), contradicting PD.)
是的。正定矩阵所有特征值 > 0 ⇒ 行列式 > 0 ⇒ 可逆。若奇异则存在非零向量使 \(x^\top A x = 0\)，与正定性矛盾。
Q17
Typical equations for circles, ellipses, hyperbolas, parabolas.
  • Circle: \(x^2 + y^2 = r^2\).
  • Ellipse: \(\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1\).
  • Hyperbola: \(\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1\) (opens L/R) or \(-\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1\) (opens U/D).
  • Parabola: \(y = a x^2\) or \(x = a y^2\).
圆：\(x^2 + y^2 = r^2\)；椭圆：\(\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1\)；双曲线：\(\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1\)；抛物线：\(y = a x^2\)。
Q18
Rewrite \(a x^2 + b x y + c y^2 = \alpha\) using a symmetric matrix.
Let \(u = (x, y)^\top\) and \(A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b/2 \\ b/2 & c \end{pmatrix}\). Then \[ u^\top A u = (x, y) \begin{pmatrix} a & b/2 \\ b/2 & c \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = a x^2 + b x y + c y^2 = \alpha. \] The quadratic form is \(Q(u) = u^\top A u\).
令 \(u = (x, y)^\top\)，\(A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b/2 \\ b/2 & c \end{pmatrix}\)，则 \(u^\top A u = a x^2 + b x y + c y^2 = \alpha\)。
Q19
Conditions on eigenvalues of \(A\) and \(\alpha\) for ellipse / hyperbola.
\(u^\top A u = \alpha\), with symmetric \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{2 \times 2}\).
  • Ellipse: Eigenvalues have the same sign (both positive or both negative), and \(\alpha\) has that same sign. After diagonalization: \(\lambda_1 \tilde{x}^2 + \lambda_2 \tilde{y}^2 = \alpha\) → \(\frac{\tilde{x}^2}{\alpha/\lambda_1} + \frac{\tilde{y}^2}{\alpha/\lambda_2} = 1\) (ellipse when both denominators positive).
  • Hyperbola: Eigenvalues have opposite signs. One squared term is positive, the other negative, giving a hyperbola regardless of \(\alpha \neq 0\).
  • Parabola: One eigenvalue = 0 (degenerate case).
\(u^\top A u = \alpha\)：椭圆要求特征值同号且 \(\alpha\) 同号；双曲线要求特征值异号；抛物线与特征值为零的退化情形对应。

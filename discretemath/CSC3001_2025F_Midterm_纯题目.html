第 1 题 · 整数表示16 分

Show that \(\mathbb{Z} = \{3s + 5t \mid s, t \in \mathbb{Z}\}\), where \(\mathbb{Z}\) denotes the set of integers.

第 2 题 · 集合包含与差集16 分

Let \(A, B, C\) be sets. Prove or disprove: If \(A \subseteq B\), \(a \in A\), \(a \notin (B - C)\), then \(a \in C\).

第 3 题 · 递推关系建模20 分

Express the following as recurrence relations and initial conditions. It is not required to solve the recurrence.

(a) (10 points) Count the number of \(n\)-bit binary strings where every occurrence of \(1\) is immediately followed by at least two \(0\)s.

(b) (10 points) Count the number of \(n\)-bit binary strings with at most two \(1\)s.

第 4 题 · 因子分解16 分

Let \(N\) be an odd composite number that is not a prime power. Let \(a\) be an integer with \(2 \le a < N\), and \(r\) be the smallest positive integer satisfying the congruence \[ a^{r} \equiv 1 \pmod{N}. \] Assume that \(a\) and \(N\) are picked in a way that \(r\) is an even number.

(a) (8 points) Show that \(N \nmid a^{r/2} - 1\).

(b) (8 points) Show that if \(\gcd(N, a^{r/2}+1) \neq N\), then \(\gcd(N, a^{r/2}-1)\) is a nontrivial factor of \(N\) (a factor of \(N\) that is not \(1\) or \(N\) itself).

第 5 题 · 斐波那契恒等式16 分

Let \(F_n\) denote the \(n\)-th Fibonacci number defined by \(F_0 = 0\), \(F_1 = 1\), and \(F_n = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2}\) for \(n \ge 2\).

(a) (8 points) Prove that for any positive integers \(m\) and \(n\), \(F_{m+n} = F_m F_{n+1} + F_n F_{m-1}\).

(b) (8 points) Prove that for integers \(m, n > 1\), \(\gcd(F_m, F_n) = F_{\gcd(m,n)}\).

第 6 题 · 二进制串翻牌游戏16 分

A \(2000\)-bit binary string has all its bits initially \(1\). In each step, Alice could pick a bit from the first \(1951\) bits that is a \(1\), and toggle that bit and the next \(49\) bits to its right (toggle means change \(1\) to \(0\) and \(0\) to \(1\)). A consecutive block of \(50\) bits will have their value changed as a consequence in each step. Alice will keep playing the game until such a bit cannot be found.

(a) (8 points) Write, from left to right, the bit string as a binary number, ignoring the leading zeros. Show that this binary number decreases in each step.

(b) (8 points) Show that regardless of Alice's strategy, the game terminates in an even number of steps.
